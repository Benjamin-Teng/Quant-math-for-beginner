### 1 (major, independent reviewer)

- **Where:** §4 pitfall "A split of the form, not of the assets", l.225: "…and the rows of [[ex:grid]], split the quadratic form, not the risk of each asset. A row of [[ex:grid]] is not Semiconductor's share of the risk."
- **Problem:** Each row of Exhibit 5 sums to A·w_i(Σ̂w)_i. That sum is exactly the Euler variance contribution that LM31 (syllabus: "Euler risk contributions") will present as asset i's share, so the sentence teaches the opposite of what LM31 will teach. The brief only forbids calling a row a contribution; it does not ask the module to deny it.
- **Correction:** Delete "and the rows of [[ex:grid]]," and the second sentence. End the pitfall with: "Splitting $s_p^2$ into one share per asset, and judging which split is meaningful, is the subject of [[LM27]] and [[LM31]]; this module stops at the gradient (Section 7)."
- **Evidence:** Unrounded row sums of Exhibit 5: 198.08, 83.49 and 39.05 %². These are 61.8%, 26.0% and 12.2% of 320.62, and each equals 10⁴·A·w_i(Σ̂w)_i.
