## Reviewer's findings (verbatim, condensed formatting)

**Critical**
- C1. L598 "14.21 bp, or 40.40 index points" (the lab prints the same) uses the wrong conversion. The module defines the move as TR/S rising by 1/(1−δ), giving δ = 1−e^(−0.0014215) = 14.20 bp of the 10 Dec close (28,400.73), which is 40.34 points — exactly the published figure (udn: 40.34 for the two stocks going ex; TSMC alone 40.26). S_prev(e^Δ−1) mixes two conventions.
- C2. L779 "bisection halves the error at each step" is false: it halves the bracket, i.e. the error bound. Exhibit 6 shows the error rising from step 3 to 4 (midpoint 1.875% is 0.066 pp off, 1.5625% is 0.246 pp off). LM4 says "midpoint error need not fall every step."

**Major**
- M1. On 27 June the December contract shows the most negative residual (−672.9) and implied financing rate (−4.44%) of all 146 days; the near-month residual that day (−258) is the 2nd most negative. On 30 June the December numbers are residual −370.5, financing −1.63%, implied qτ at r=0 2.45%. L687 "Even at r=0, the contract implied more dividends… than the 2.93% paid in all of 2025" holds on only 17 of 44 June–July days and on no day from 30 June onward; "even at r=0, F was below fair value given realized dividends" is true on 43 of 44 days. Put a multi-day figure in the overview (L25), summary (L782) and 導讀 (L695) — a centred 23 Jun–3 Jul mean is −461 — and label 27 June as the extreme day.
- M2. 30 June pitfall (L690): TAIEX −324.06 (−1.435%), July TX +22 (+0.10%) are right, but 17.8 bp (about 40 points) of the index fall was ex-dividend; the total-return index fell only 1.26%, and by the KC at L584 an ex-date raises the basis by the dividend. The real timing noise is about 306 points: the July residual swung from −258.3 to +49.3, and TAIEX rebounded +297.7 on 1 July. Say that the TAIEX close is a 13:25–13:30 call-auction price while the TX daily settlement is the VWAP of 13:44–13:45.
- M3. Volume on 27 June: Sep 22 lots, Dec 49, Mar26 9, Jun26 2. Last trade vs settlement: Jun26 21,439 vs 21,493; Sep 21,800 vs 21,834. December has a median of 14.5 lots a day, four days with no trades, and last-trade/settlement gaps up to 256 points. On 30 June Jun26 did not trade yet printed a settlement of 21,501. This affects Exhibits 1, 11, 13, 14, the calendar-spread test (L629), the "fixed at the same moment" KC answer (L701) and the claim that the residual grows with maturity. Add volumes and a caveat.
- M4. S·e^{rτ}(1−e^{−qτ}) values the realized dividend yields forward from 27 June; the index points actually removed by ex-dividends from 28 Jun to 17 Dec were 440.7 (444.0 valued at expiry), not 422.24, because TAIEX rose 22%. (6.23) says dividends "paid at times t_j" (L557) and the text says "paid" throughout, but the total-return index measures ex-dates; TSMC's 11 Dec dividend counts for the December contract but is paid in January 2026.
- M5. i, v periodic rate (simple annual in (6.21)), v = 1/(1+i); g simple per-period growth (L124, L240–331, g₂) vs book g = log growth (the note at L56 says "Sections 4–6", but g appears in Section 2 and not in 5–6); k required return and an index (a_k, B_k, P_k, "k halvings"); r, q claimed consistent with the book's r though the book's r is a log return and the risk-free rate is r_f; L loan principal (L435–477) vs book L; f_{A,B−A} forward rate vs f, with A and B dates but A also the payment; F face value (L345), FX forward (6.21) and futures price; P price but P_k principal portion (6.18); s_{n,i} vs book s; T in (6.21) and PP14 vs τ from (6.22) on.
