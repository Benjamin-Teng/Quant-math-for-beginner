  - **Fit.** A Nelder–Mead refinement of `t.fit` (tolerances $10^{-12}$) and an independent L-BFGS-B run from (5, 0, 1) agree: $\nu=3.0652571$, location 0.0997260%, scale 0.7761160%.

  - **The in-model ratio.**
    - The fit's standard deviation is 1.316536%, so in that model $\operatorname{SE}(\bar R)=0.017214\%$.
