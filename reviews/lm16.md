## Adversarial check of the major findings

**1 — confirmed.** Line 204 lists three identities and then says "The last line needs only zero covariances". The third identity, $\Var(R_1-R_2)=\sigma_1^2+\sigma_2^2-2\sigma_{12}$, holds for any covariance, so the sentence can only mean (16.3)'s last sentence, which sits two paragraphs up, across the proof. The brief's §4 row ("uncorrelated suffices (LM3's increments)") confirms that intent.

**2 — confirmed.** Own code: the mean of $d_t^2$ is 2.000000 for the pair, 5.000000 for S5 and 28.000000 for U28, as LM12 proves for every sample by the trace (lm12.md:455–459). The population counterpart needs no normality: by (16.4), $\mathbf W=L^{-1}(\mathbf X-\boldsymbol\mu)$ has covariance matrix $L^{-1}\Sigma L^{-\top}=I$, so $\E d^2=\E\lVert\mathbf W\rVert^2=N$ for any law with $\Sigma$ positive definite. The note therefore misstates what row 7's mean-$N$ fact needs. The reviewer's split is right; (16.4) gives it without the trace, which LM16 does not introduce.

**3 — partly.** SOURCES confirms B&H's §3.8 "Independence of rvs" and §7.5 "Multivariate Normal" only as contents entries; its "Read in the text" list for the free edition names neither, and TODO's Stage 2 hand-back leaves "LM16 cites Blitzstein and Hwang §3.8 and §7.5 by section" open. The book's order in SOURCES (§5.3 universality of the Uniform, §7.2 2D LOTUS) puts continuous random variables in chapter 5 and joint densities in chapter 7, so §3.8 cannot state the density case. The replacement is right but mis-numbered: in the CHANCE PDF (§4.2), Definition 4.7 defines mutual independence of continuous random variables by the joint distribution function, and Theorem 4.2 reads "these random variables are mutually independent if and only if f(x1, …, xn) = f1(x1)f2(x2)⋯fn(xn)"; Theorem 4.3 says that continuous functions of mutually independent variables are mutually independent.

**4 — confirmed.** Lines 312–439 (§6) contain no statement that an entry or a sub-vector of a normal vector is normal; "margins" appears only in the pitfall about the converse (lines 428–429). The fact follows from the definition with $\mathbf a$ a unit vector, or from (16.7) with $M$ made of rows of the identity, but the module never says so. Outcome 5 (line 14) and the brief's "Owns" both list marginals.

**5 — partly.** The 導讀's "常態模型的尾部太薄" turns 177 against 48.68 into a property of the normal model: the reading that E8 (line 386) leaves to [[LM21]] and that decision C26 excludes. E9's verb alone is the volume's descriptive usage for an in-sample fit (lm15.md:171: "misses the bad ones: 9 months … against 0.123"), but "The model misses the whole tail, since …" argues from the comparison. The rewording keeps every number, drops the verdict and is shorter.
