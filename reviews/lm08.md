## Findings in full

### 1 (major, independent reviewer)

- **Where:** §3 'The Dot Product' (rendered numbering), pitfall 'A limit-locked P&L is a mark, not cash', line 150: 'So the TX line's +425,800 is a mark at a price where almost nobody could trade.' Also 中文導讀 '內積就是損益', line 153: '幾乎沒有人能在那個價位成交'.
- **Problem:** The market mechanics are wrong for this book's TX leg. In a locked limit-down market, sell orders queue at the limit and any buy order fills at once; the 2,736 contracts that traded were such buys. So the short TX line could have bought back at 19,167 and locked in +425,800. The mark nobody could realize is the long 0050 line at 158.40, where sellers could not trade. The brief's wording ('few could trade at the settlement') has the same slip.
- **Correction:** Replace the two sentences with: 'So the settlement of 19,167 rests on 2,736 contracts. In a locked limit-down market, sell orders queue while a buy order fills at once. The short TX line could have bought back at 19,167 and locked in +425,800, but the long 0050 line could not be sold at 158.40, TWSE's 10% limit. The book could not be closed at its marks, so treat a limit day's P&L as a valuation, not as cash.' 導讀: '跌停鎖死時，賣單排隊賣不掉，買單卻能立刻成交：期貨空單可以在 19,167 回補、實現獲利，0050 多單卻無法在 158.40 賣出，所以整本帳的損益只是評價。'
- **Evidence:** Raw TX 202504 on 2025-04-07: close = settlement = 19,167, the first tick inside 0.9×21,296 = 19,166.4; volume 2,736 against 66,423 on 2 April. The TAIFEX TX page (fetched) confirms the ±10% limit off the previous regular session's settlement, the last-minute VWAP settlement and the 1-point tick.
