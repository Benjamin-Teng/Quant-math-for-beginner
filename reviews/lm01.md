## Reviewer's findings (verbatim, condensed formatting)

1. Line 246, §3: "The continuously compounded rate equivalent to an EAR is simply its natural logarithm." Read literally this says r = ln(EAR) (e.g. ln 0.0174 = −4.05) and contradicts the pitfall at line 123. Fix: "…is the natural logarithm of one plus the EAR, r = ln(1+R)."
2. Lines 405–415, eq. (1.14) and the weighted AM–GM statement state no conditions: weights must be w_i ≥ 0 and sum to 1, and the equality case needs every w_i > 0. w_i = V_{i,0}/V_0 can be negative for a short or levered position. Counterexample: w = (1.5, −0.5), r = (0.10, 0.20) gives r_p = ln(1.5e^{0.1} − 0.5e^{0.2}) = 0.0460 < 0.0500. The same overclaim is in the result box (l.445), the pitfall (l.449) and the zh box (l.452, "一定低估").
3. D means the dividend (l.109–117, 279, 583), trading days per year (l.329–347, 566, 720) and drawdown (l.25, 499–556, 634, 748). N is both the number of sessions (1.11) and of positions (1.13). T is a number of periods in (1.1), (1.12) and the HPR product (l.280) but years in (1.9), (1.10), (1.16).
4. Line 320: in GBM the drift μ is not g; g = μ − σ²/2. Fix: "…g is the growth rate of log wealth (in LM29, μ − σ²/2), the quantity Kelly maximizes in LM42."
5. Lines 114–116: "1+R_t = (P_t+D_t)/P_{t−1}" is false on 2025-06-18 unless P_{t−1} is restated per new share (P_{t−1}/4); multiplying P_t/P_{t−1} literally gives 1.7603 = G_P/4, not 7.0413.
6. Line 144: "log₂ 數「翻了幾倍」" reads as "how many-fold"; use "數「翻了幾番」（翻倍幾次）".
7. Line 167: 1.725% is the 定期儲蓄存款 fixed rate; the one-year 定期存款 fixed rate posted the same day is 1.700%. The doubling exhibit (l.477) has the same problem.
8. Line 127: on a log axis the height is an affine function of ln W; the straight line comes from ln(W₀e^{gt}) = ln W₀ + gt, not from the quotient rule.
9. Line 290: "For T>1 the exponent 1/T shrinks the growth factor" is wrong when G < 1; say "pulls it toward 1".
10. Line 95: write ln[(1+R)^T].
11. Line 449: say "weighted averages with start-of-period weights".
12. Line 483: the rules cap the rate at 15% and forbid compounding; they do not impose 15%.
13. Line 523: troughs end the declines; the episodes end at recovery.
14. Line 173: 1.075² − 1 = 15.5625% exactly, which rounds to 15.563%.
15. The case uses days/365.25 but (1.11) and the 90-day example use 365/h (49.03% vs 48.99%); "about 244" vs "252 or 243?" vs LM3's "about 243".
16. Lines 19, 141: the "April 2025 decline" runs from 7 January to 9 April; only −16.93% of the −27.49% came in April.
17. Definition 1.5 and proposition 1.6 use limits before LM2 with no pointer; the monotone convergence statement in the Deep Dive is not labelled *stated without proof*.
18. Line 584: `px.join(div, …, how="left")` then `.shift(1)` relies on row order (not guaranteed in Polars 2.0); cum_max ignores W₀ = 1; the code's DD sign is the opposite of the text's.
19. Line 623: the 2025-06-18 return covers six market sessions (halt 11–17 June).
20. References: PTS headline and date; cite MacTutor directly; Stewart edition and year; Art. 48 from 全國法規資料庫.
21. Exhibits 6, 7, 9 and 10 are never cited in the prose.
