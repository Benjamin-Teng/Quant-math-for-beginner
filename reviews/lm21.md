### Reviewer's M1 (now m1): downgraded to minor

**The reviewer's claim.** LM20 "never gives a variance's standard error".

**What LM20 and LM18 print.**

- lm20.md:358 gives $\Var(s^2)\approx(\operatorname{Kurt}-1)\sigma^4/T$. At Kurt = 3 its square root is $\sigma^2\sqrt{2/T}$.
- lm20.md:308 gives $\operatorname{SE}(\hat\sigma)=s/\sqrt{2T}$. The delta method (20.8) with $h(\sigma)=\sigma^2$ turns this into the same $\sigma^2\sqrt{2/T}$.
- lm18.md:150 states the relative standard error $\sqrt{2/n}$ for near-normal draws.

So the formula is supported one step from LM20's printed text; only the label "LM20's standard error of a variance" is loose.

**Data.** The 292 block counts, from the first 5,840 returns:

- variance 4.71749 (divisor 292);
- $\hat p=0.547945$ and $20\hat p\hat q=4.95403$;
- sample kurtosis 2.630, against 2.902 for $\operatorname{Bin}(20,\hat p)$. The counts are nearly normal.

**The z values do not change the decision.**

| Inputs | Against 4.95 | Against 5.16 |
|:--|--:|--:|
| Printed values, as the key | −0.5614 | −1.0303 |
