- **M1 (major, independent reviewer): confirmed.** I read the CHANCE PDF (version of 4 July 2006), §5.2, subsections "Functions of a Random Variable" and "Simulation" (pp. 210–212). Theorem 5.1 states F_Y(y)=F_X(φ⁻¹(y)) for a strictly increasing φ of a continuous X. The simulation subsection derives the inverse transform from it and states Corollary 5.2: "If F(y) is a given cumulative distribution function that is strictly increasing when 0 < F(y) < 1 and if U is a random variable with uniform distribution on [0, 1], then Y = F⁻¹(U) has the cumulative distribution F(y)." The book therefore covers only the strictly increasing case, and under the Corollary's number. The text attributes the general case to it. Glasserman §2.2.1 does cover the general case: Google Books snippets of pp. 54–55 (e9GWUsQkPNMC, read 2026-10-18) give "we may set F⁻¹(u) = inf{x : F(x) ≥ u} (2.14)" and "Fig. 2.6. Inverse transform for distributions with jumps (left) or flat sections (right)". The error began in project/SOURCES.md, whose Grinstead and Snell entry records "inverse transform §5.2 Theorem 5.1". A citation must exist as cited (owner's rule), so the finding stays major.
