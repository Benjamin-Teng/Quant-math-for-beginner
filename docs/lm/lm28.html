Minimize variance for a target mean in closed form, find the best Sharpe ratio beside a risk-free asset, and measure how far the answers move with their inputs.

1Introduction

LM27 took the weights w as given and computed a portfolio's mean, variance and risk contributions. This module chooses w: the least variance for a target mean, and the best Sharpe ratio beside a risk-free asset. It also states when each answer exists and how far it moves with its inputs. Choosing weights by mean and variance began with Markowitz (1952).

Motivating CaseFive funds, two years, one optimizer

At the end of February 2019 a TWD investor holds five Taiwan-listed funds. They track Taiwan stocks (0050), US stocks (00646), long US Treasuries (00679B), gold futures (00635U) and crude-oil futures (00642U). The investor has 504 days of joint returns, from 13 February 2017 to 27 February 2019. Which mix has the least variance, and which the best Sharpe ratio?

On those days the calculus is exact. The minimum-variance mix holds every fund, 41.4% in the bond fund, and predicts 5.02% volatility a year. At a 1.5% risk-free rate the best Sharpe ratio is 0.88, at 7.81% volatility, with oil sold short (Exhibit 1).

Hold both mixes from 4 March 2019 to 8 October 2026. The first realizes 10.05%, twice its prediction; the second 14.15%. And one more point of expected return on gold, under a sixth of its standard error, would lift gold's weight from 2.6% to 12.8%. Was the calculus wrong, were the weights wrong, or did the inputs change?

The first window was calm. Its minimum volatility ranks third lowest of the through case's 92 windows, whose minima run from 4.97% (31 May 2019) to 10.42% (31 March 2022).

Sections 2 to 4 derive the minimum-variance portfolio, the frontier and the tangency portfolio. Section 5 measures how the weights react to the means, Section 6 holds the first window's portfolios to the end, and Section 7 computes in Python.

2The Minimum-Variance Portfolio

derive the minimum-variance portfolio of N assets from the Lagrangian, and state when it exists and is unique

LM10 showed that a portfolio's variance w⊤Σw is a convex function of the weights, and stopped at its gradient. Minimizing it under linear constraints takes one Lagrangian, as in LM7, now with N assets and k constraints at once.

Let Σ be an N×N positive definite matrix, K a k×N matrix of full row rank k, and b a vector of length k. The feasible set {w:Kw=b} is not empty, because K has independent rows. The budget alone is K=1⊤ with b=1.

With LM7's signs the Lagrangian is L=w⊤Σw−λ⊤(Kw−b). By (10.8) its gradient in w is 2Σw−K⊤λ, so a critical point has w=21​Σ−1K⊤λ, and the constraint then requires 21​KΣ−1K⊤λ=b. The matrix KΣ−1K⊤ is positive definite. By (11.8) the eigenvalues of Σ−1 are the reciprocals of Σ's, all positive, and K⊤λ=0 whenever λ=0, because K⊤ has independent columns. Hence every λ=0 has

λ⊤KΣ−1K⊤λ=(K⊤λ)⊤Σ−1(K⊤λ)>0,

so KΣ−1K⊤λ=0. The matrix is therefore invertible, and the multipliers are unique.

TheoremMinimum variance under linear constraints

Let Σ be positive definite and K (k×N) of full row rank. Then the problem minw⊤Σw subject to Kw=b has the unique solution w∗, with value and multipliers

w∗=Σ−1K⊤(KΣ−1K⊤)−1b,w∗⊤Σw∗=b⊤(KΣ−1K⊤)−1b,λ=2(KΣ−1K⊤)−1b.​(28.1)
Every feasible w satisfies
w⊤Σw​=w∗⊤Σw∗+(w−w∗)⊤Σ(w−w∗).​

Proof. Let w be feasible and Δ:=w−w∗, so that KΔ=0. By (10.9),

w⊤Σw=w∗⊤Σw∗+2Δ⊤Σw∗+Δ⊤ΣΔ.

The middle term is Δ⊤K⊤λ=(KΔ)⊤λ=0, because 2Σw∗=K⊤λ. The last term is positive unless Δ=0, since Σ is positive definite. So w∗ is the unique minimum, and substituting it gives the value. ■

The identity proves existence and uniqueness by itself, so no KKT theory is needed. The result is also LM10's theorem at work: a positive definite Σ makes the variance strictly convex. Long-only weights add inequality constraints, which need the KKT conditions of LM7's (7.13). LM29 applies them.

With the budget alone, KΣ−1K⊤=1⊤Σ−11 is a number, and (28.1) becomes the following result.

ResultThe global minimum-variance portfolio

For Σ positive definite, the fully invested portfolio of least variance is

wmv=1⊤Σ−11Σ−11​,σmin2​=1⊤Σ−111​,λ∗=2σmin2​,Σwmv=σmin2​1.​(28.2)
For every w with 1⊤w=1,
w⊤Σw​=σmin2​+(w−wmv)⊤Σ(w−wmv).​(28.3)

Identity (28.3) is LM7's (7.9) for N assets, and λ∗ is its shadow price of the budget. The last part of (28.2) says that every fund has covariance σmin2​ with wmv. So does every fully invested portfolio, since w⊤Σwmv=σmin2​1⊤w. By (27.5) every fund's beta to wmv is 1, and by (27.6) every risk share equals its weight. LM11 solved Σ^x=1; rescaled to sum to 1, its solution is (28.2).

Solution

PitfallSolve, never invert, and test positive definiteness first

Compute Σ−11 with np.linalg.solve(S, ones), which factorizes once and rounds less than forming the inverse. Before that, np.linalg.cholesky(S) tests positive definiteness: a symmetric matrix is positive definite exactly when it has a Cholesky factor (LM12). If the factor fails, some nonzero weight vector has zero or negative variance under Σ. Then (28.1) no longer applies, and a unique minimum need not exist.

導讀最小變異數：一個線性方程組

在權重總和為 1 的條件下讓變異數（variance）最小，用的就是 LM7 的拉格朗日乘數（Lagrange multiplier）法，只是資產從兩檔變成 N 檔。共變異數矩陣（covariance matrix）Σ 正定（positive definite）時，變異數是嚴格凸函數（strictly convex function），所以最小值至多只有一個，這就是 LM10 所說最佳化的基礎。最小變異數投資組合（minimum-variance portfolio）是把 Σx=1 的解縮放成總和為 1，每一檔基金與它的共變異數都等於 σmin2​。恆等式（identity）(28.3) 直接證明它就是全域最小值（global minimum）：最小值存在，而且只有這一個，不需要 KKT 理論。E5 第一個視窗（window）的最小變異數投資組合五檔都持有，預測波動率（volatility）5.02%，低於任何一檔基金。

3The Frontier and Two-Fund Separation

derive the frontier as a parabola in variance and mean, and explain two-fund separation

Now add a target mean. A frontier portfolio has the least variance among fully invested portfolios with mean μp​: (28.1) with K=(1 μ)⊤ and b=(1,μp​)⊤. The two rows are independent exactly when μ is not proportional to 1.

A shorter route starts from wmv. Write w=wmv+Δ with 1⊤Δ=0. By (28.3) the variance is σmin2​+Δ⊤ΣΔ, and the mean is μmv​+(μ−μmv​1)⊤Δ. So Δ solves (28.1) with the rows 1⊤ and (μ−μmv​1)⊤ and the right-hand side (0,μp​−μmv​)⊤. Its 2×2 matrix is diagonal, because (28.2) gives 1⊤Σ−1μ=μmv​/σmin2​ and 1⊤Σ−11=1/σmin2​:

1⊤Σ−1(μ−μmv​1)=σmin2​μmv​​−σmin2​μmv​​=0.

The inverse of that diagonal matrix follows from (9.5) at once, and (28.1) gives the frontier.

ResultThe minimum-variance frontier

Let Σ be positive definite and μ not proportional to 1, and let SR∞2​:=(μ−μmv​1)⊤Σ−1(μ−μmv​1)>0. The fully invested portfolio of least variance with mean μp​ is

w(μp​)​=wmv+SR∞2​μp​−μmv​​Σ−1(μ−μmv​1),​(28.4)
and its variance is
σp2​=σmin2​+SR∞2​(μp​−μmv​)2​.(28.5)

Over all targets, the frontier portfolios form the minimum-variance frontier. Equation (28.5) is a parabola in (σp2​,μp​) and a hyperbola in (σp​,μp​) with vertex (σmin​,μmv​). As σp​ grows, it approaches the asymptotes μp​=μmv​±SR∞​σp​ (Problem 8). The upper half, μp​≥μmv​, is the efficient frontier: for each variance it holds the higher mean.

The multiplier of the target mean is a shadow price, (7.6): by (28.5), λμ​=dσp2​/dμp​=2(μp​−μmv​)/SR∞2​. Deep Dive 1 computes both multipliers from the 2×2 system.

By (28.4) the frontier weights are affine in μp​. So any two distinct frontier portfolios wa​ and wb​, with means μa​ and μb​, rebuild every other:

w(μp​)=wa​+μb​−μa​μp​−μa​​(wb​−wa​).

The rebuilding is two-fund separation: an investor who wants a frontier portfolio needs only two funds. Merton (1972) derived the frontier in closed form and proved this separation as a mutual-fund theorem.

導讀最小變異數前緣是一條拋物線

指定目標平均報酬（mean return）之後，變異數（variance）最小的投資組合構成最小變異數前緣（minimum-variance frontier）。以變異數為橫軸、平均數（mean）為縱軸時它是一條拋物線（parabola），換成波動率（volatility）為橫軸則是雙曲線（hyperbola），頂點是最小變異數投資組合（minimum-variance portfolio），兩條漸近線（asymptote）的斜率是 ±SR∞​。前緣上的權重是目標平均數的一次函數，所以任選兩個前緣投資組合，就能組合出其他所有的前緣投資組合：這就是兩基金分離（two-fund separation），是分離定理（separation theorem）的一種。上半支才是效率前緣（efficient frontier），上面的投資組合是效率投資組合（efficient portfolio）。第一個視窗（window）的等權重投資組合落在前緣內側，平均數比最小變異數投資組合低，波動卻更高。

4The Tangency Portfolio and the Capital Market Line

derive the tangency portfolio and the capital market line, and state when the tangency portfolio exists
compute a risk-averse investor's exposures beside a risk-free asset, and relate them to the tangency portfolio

Now add a risk-free asset that earns rf​. A book holds exposures f to the funds and cash 1−L at rf​, where L=1⊤f, so cash is borrowing when L>1. By (8.4) its return is rf​+f⊤(R−rf​1), with mean rf​+f⊤e and, by (16.4), variance f⊤Σf. Borrowing without limit at the risk-free rate is an idealization. LM29 adds a financing spread and limits.

The least-variance book with mean μp​=rf​ solves (28.1) with K=e⊤ and b=μp​−rf​, for any e=0:

f∗=e⊤Σ−1e(μp​−rf​)Σ−1e​,SR∗2:=e⊤Σ−1e,f∗⊤Σf∗=SR∗2(μp​−rf​)2​.​

So every least-variance book is a multiple of Σ−1e, and every book has ∣μp​−rf​∣≤SR∗σp​. The least-variance books with μp​>rf​, the efficient ones, lie on the capital market line μp​=rf​+SR∗σp​.

A fully invested book on that direction divides Σ−1e by 1⊤Σ−1e. By (28.2), that sum is (μmv​−rf​)/σmin2​, so its sign is the sign of μmv​−rf​.

ResultThe tangency portfolio and the capital market line

Let Σ be positive definite, μ not proportional to 1, and rf​<μmv​. The fully invested portfolio with the highest Sharpe ratio is

wtan=1⊤Σ−1eΣ−1e​,1⊤Σ−1e=σmin2​μmv​−rf​​>0,SR∗2=e⊤Σ−1e,​(28.6)
with mean and volatility
μtan​−rf​=μmv​−rf​SR∗2σmin2​​,σtan​=μmv​−rf​SR∗σmin2​​.​
Its Sharpe ratio decomposes, and the capital market line passes through it:
SR∗2=SR∞2​+σmin2​(μmv​−rf​)2​,μp​=rf​+SR∗σp​.​(28.7)

The portfolio wtan is the book on the direction Σ−1e with L=1, and its excess mean SR∗2/(1⊤Σ−1e) is positive. So it lies on the capital market line and attains SR∗, and its mean and volatility follow by substitution. Problem 8 derives (28.7) by splitting e.

Every efficient book is a multiple of Σ−1e, so the mix of risky funds does not depend on how much of the book they take. Tobin (1958, read in Cowles Foundation Discussion Paper No. 14, 1956, §3.6) found this separation with risk-free cash at zero yield, no borrowing, and non-negativity assumed but not binding. Equation (28.6) allows any rf​<μmv​ and borrowing at rf​, and on the first window it shorts oil, which his non-negativity excludes.

By (28.6), Σwtan=e/(1⊤Σ−1e). So by (27.5) every fund's beta to the tangency portfolio is proportional to its excess return:

βi,tan​=μtan​−rf​ei​​,that is,μi​−rf​=βi,tan​(μtan​−rf​).​

The second form is the CAPM's equation of LM23, E[Ri​]−rf​=βi​(E[Rmkt​]−rf​), with wtan in the market's place. Conversely, a fully invested portfolio with μp​=rf​ whose betas satisfy it for every fund is the normalized Σ−1e. The identity holds on any data, so it is no evidence for the CAPM. Why the market should be the tangency portfolio is a question of equilibrium theory, outside this book.

ResultRisk aversion with a risk-free asset

Let δ>0 and Σ be positive definite, with borrowing and lending at rf​ without limit. Then

fmax​ f⊤e−2δ​f⊤Σfhas the unique solutionf∗=δΣ−1e​,​(28.8)
with volatility SR∗/δ. When rf​<μmv​, the solution is f∗=Lwtan with L=1⊤Σ−1e/δ>0.

The objective's Hessian is −δΣ, so the objective is strictly concave for any rf​ (LM10), and its one critical point is the maximum. By (10.8) that point solves e−δΣf=0, and its variance is e⊤Σ−1e/δ2. The investor therefore holds the tangency mix, levered when L>1 and diluted with cash when L<1, and δ chooses only how much. LM7 found the same structure for two assets: its Kelly fractions are L∗wtan. The Kelly fractions of N assets and growth-optimal leverage are LM43's.

導讀切點投資組合與資本市場線

加入無風險資產（risk-free asset）之後，所有效率投資組合（efficient portfolio）的曝險（exposure）都和 Σ−1e 同方向，只差規模大小。把這個方向縮放成權重總和為 1，就是切點投資組合（tangency portfolio），它的夏普比率（Sharpe ratio）最高。從無風險利率（risk-free rate）出發、穿過切點的直線，就是資本市場線（capital market line）。前提是最小變異數投資組合（minimum-variance portfolio）的平均報酬（mean return）高於 rf​，低於 rf​ 時同一個公式算出的是夏普比率最低的投資組合，等於 rf​ 時則無法縮放（分母為零）。風險規避（risk aversion）係數 δ 只決定持有多少切點投資組合，δ 越大，現金越多。每檔基金對切點投資組合的 beta 都與它的超額報酬（excess return）成正比，這在任何資料上都成立，所以不能拿來驗證 CAPM。

5How Tangency Weights React to the Means

compute how tangency weights respond to one mean, and compare that change with the mean's standard error
explain why unconstrained mean–variance weights are in-sample estimates, not recommendations

The means in (28.6) are estimates. Exposures respond to them linearly: by (28.8), a change Δe moves the exposures by exactly Σ−1Δe/δ. By (11.8), Σ−1 divides each eigenvector's part of Δe by its eigenvalue, so a change along a direction of small variance moves the book the most.

The weights also divide by 1⊤Σ−1e, which moves too. A Jacobian matrix stacks as rows the gradients of several functions, each as in (10.8). Differentiating (28.6) by the quotient rule gives the weights' Jacobian, with entry (i,j) equal to ∂witan​/∂ej​:

Δf∗=δΣ−1Δe​ exactly,∂e⊤∂wtan​=1⊤Σ−1e(I−wtan1⊤)Σ−1​.​(28.9)

The Jacobian gives only a first-order approximation of a weight change. Exhibit 4 shows the exact changes on the first window: its diagonal runs from +1.9 points (oil) through +4.7 (00646), +6.6 (0050) and +7.6 (00679B) to +10.3 (gold).

The exposures change exactly by column 4 of Σ−1 times 0.01/δ: (−2.0,+3.2,−3.6,+14.6,−0.9) points, which sum to an 11.3-point rise in L.

Two years of means cannot fix tangency weights to the nearest 10 points: a change of 0.15 standard errors in gold's mean moves its weight by 10.3. Shrinking the means or the covariance matrix is the repair of LM30.

Best and Grauer (1991) find that with only a budget constraint an efficient portfolio's weights, mean and variance can be extremely sensitive to the means. LM30 measures how much more errors in means cost than errors in covariances.

導讀平均數差一點，權重差很多

曝險（exposure）Σ−1e/δ 對平均報酬（mean return）是線性的，但切點投資組合（tangency portfolio）的權重還要除以 1⊤Σ−1e，所以是非線性的。敏感度（sensitivity）由 Σ−1 決定：沿著變異數（variance）小的方向，平均數（mean）的一點變動會被放大很多。第一個視窗（window）中，黃金的平均報酬只要提高 1 個百分點，也就是標準誤（standard error）的 0.15 倍，它的權重就從 2.6% 升到 12.8%。兩年資料估出的平均數，估計誤差（estimation error）遠大於這個變動，所以樣本內（in-sample）算出的切點權重不能當成建議。LM30 討論修正的方法。

7Mean–Variance in Python

compute frontier portfolios in NumPy from linear systems, and check them and their multipliers with cvxpy

The KKT system of (28.1) stacks stationarity, 2Σw=K⊤λ, and feasibility into one linear system:

(2ΣK​K⊤0​)(w−λ​)=(0b​).
Python
import cvxpy as cp
import numpy as np

# X: the first window's 504 x 5 daily returns; A = 242.95, rf = 0.015;
# mu_p: a target mean. Keep mu, S and rf in the same (annual) units:
# w_tan depends on them, w_mv does not.
mu = A * X.mean(axis=0)
S = A * np.cov(X, rowvar=False, ddof=0)
np.linalg.cholesky(S)                  # raises LinAlgError if not PD
one = np.ones(5)
x = np.linalg.solve(S, one)
w_mv = x / x.sum()                     # (28.2)
y = np.linalg.solve(S, mu - rf * one)
assert y.sum() > 0                     # r_f < mu_mv, (28.6)
w_tan = y / y.sum()

K = np.vstack([one, mu])               # a frontier point, (28.1)
b = np.array([1.0, mu_p])
KKT = np.block([[2 * S, K.T], [K, np.zeros((2, 2))]])
w, neg_lam = np.split(np.linalg.solve(KKT, np.r_[np.zeros(5), b]), [5])

v = cp.Variable(5)
cons = [one @ v == 1, mu @ v == mu_p]
prob = cp.Problem(cp.Minimize(cp.quad_form(v, cp.psd_wrap(S))), cons)
prob.solve(solver=cp.CLARABEL, tol_gap_abs=1e-12, tol_gap_rel=1e-12,
           tol_feas=1e-12)
print(prob.status, np.abs(v.value - w).max(), cons[0].dual_value)

The KKT system reproduces the closed forms (28.1) and (28.4) to below 10−15. cvxpy 1.9.3 then solves the problem on the annualized inputs, with a fresh Problem per solver. Knowledge Check 6

Why np.linalg.solve(S, ones) rather than np.linalg.inv(S) @ ones? And what does a failed np.linalg.cholesky(S) tell you before any solve?

Answer

A solve factorizes Σ once and rounds less than forming the inverse and multiplying. A failed Cholesky factor means that Σ is not positive definite (LM12): some nonzero weight vector has zero or negative variance, and (28.1) need not have a unique minimum.

Summary

  • For a positive definite Σ and linear constraints, minimum variance has one closed-form solution, (28.1), whose identity proves uniqueness.
  • The minimum-variance portfolio (28.2) has covariance σmin2​ with every fund. On E5's first window it predicts 5.02%.
  • The frontier (28.4)–(28.5) is a parabola in variance and mean, and any two frontier portfolios rebuild it (two-fund separation).
  • Beside a risk-free asset every efficient book is a multiple of Σ−1e. The tangency portfolio (28.6) exists only when rf​<μmv​, and δ in (28.8) chooses how much of it to hold.
  • Tangency weights are fragile: 0.15 standard errors on gold's mean move its weight by 10.3 points.
  • Held to 2026, the minimum-variance portfolio missed mostly because the minimum itself moved, from 5.02% to 9.63%. The tangency portfolio's weight error added 4.51 points more.
  • Unconstrained mean–variance weights are in-sample estimates, not recommendations. LM29 adds constraints, and LM30 repairs the inputs.

Glossary

Term 中文 Meaning
Minimum-variance portfolio 最小變異數投資組合 The fully invested portfolio of least variance, wmv, (28.2).
Minimum-variance frontier 最小變異數前緣 The fully invested portfolios of least variance for each target mean, (28.4)–(28.5).
Efficient frontier 效率前緣 The upper half of the minimum-variance frontier.
Efficient portfolio 效率投資組合 A portfolio on the efficient frontier, or, beside a risk-free asset, on the capital market line.
Two-fund separation 兩基金分離 Any two frontier portfolios rebuild the whole frontier, because its weights are affine in the target mean.
Tangency portfolio 切點投資組合 The fully invested portfolio with the highest Sharpe ratio, Σ−1e/(1⊤Σ−1e) when rf​<μmv​, (28.6).
Capital market line 資本市場線 The line μp​=rf​+SR∗σp​ of efficient books beside a risk-free asset, (28.7).
Risk-free asset 無風險資產 An asset with the certain return rf​, here an illustrative 1.5% a year.
Risk aversion 風險規避 The weight δ of the variance penalty in (28.8).
Shadow price 影子價格 A multiplier read as the rate at which the optimal value changes with its constraint, (7.6).
Sensitivity 敏感度 The change of an optimal weight for a change of an input, exact or by the Jacobian (28.9).
Jacobian matrix Jacobi 矩陣 The matrix whose rows are the gradients of several functions.
In-sample / out-of-sample 樣本內／樣本外 Data used to estimate a portfolio versus later data used to test it.
