1Introduction

LM32 measured losses by the tail of a return distribution, value at risk and expected shortfall, and by the path, drawdowns. This module asks what named and constructed shocks do to the same books, without a probability. Each shock is one more row of price changes in LM8's (8.13), and one more P&L of a fixed book.

Motivating CaseFive days, one session

After its close on 2 April 2025, Taiwan's market did not trade again until 7 April. That session the TAIEX total-return index (TR23) fell 9.70%, its worst day since 2003 (LM26). Its five sessions to 9 April lost 15.96%, second only to March 2020's 16.51%. 0050 traded at one price all day, 158.40, exactly 10% below its last close: 29.6 million units, none lower.

00646 and 00642U have no daily limit, and they fell 12.59% and 14.62% (Exhibit 1). The through case's eight rules lost from 5.06% (R7) to 8.33% (R4). R8 had raised its exposure to 1.319 on 3 March, held 1.331 at the close of 2 April and lost 7.94%. A beta model fitted on 2,362 days, that day included, gives R1 3.62%, under half the actual 7.70%.

What did each rule lose in the four other scenarios? Which shock, not seen yet, would cost a rule a tenth of its wealth? And what would a book of 0050 levered five times have lost by the next open?

Sections 2–4 replay five windows, 5–6 build shocks, 7–8 add limits, gaps and leverage, and 9 says what a stress test cannot show.

NoteResearch hygiene

Stress tests here are replays and constructions, not forecasts. The through case fixed its rules on 24 October 2026, and replays of their books are descriptive, as are the windows, limits, intraday statistics and the 2022 illustration. Betas, distances, the partial scenario, the (16.10) check and the reverse stress test use moments that include the scenario days: in-sample. The growth and drawdowns of Exhibit 9 and the lab's checkpoint are out-of-sample.

2008 and 2015 on US proxies (GLD also for 00635U, traded from 15 April 2015) and first weights are hypothetical. So are 2020 on proxies, the factor shock, ruin scalings, the TX position, presets and problems. The worst day of each fund and the break's 0.30 are constructed. Counted: eight rules, none chosen by a scenario, five scenarios, one illustration, four built shocks and one reverse stress test per book, none with a probability.

2Scenarios from History

replay a historical scenario: define its window, split the frozen and named-day losses by fund, and compare the rule's own path
explain how price limits, holidays and overnight gaps change a "one-day" loss, and which funds have no daily limit under TWSE's and TPEx's rules

A scenario fixes a return for every fund, the vector Rsc, and a book with exposures f and wealth W0​ gains W0​f⊤Rsc (LM8's (8.4)). A historical scenario takes it from a past window, and a hypothetical scenario builds it.

DefinitionScenario window

On TR23 the trough is the lowest close of the named year (2008, 2015, 2020) or of the 20 sessions from 5 August 2024 or 7 April 2025. The peak is the highest close after the previous scenario's trough, or after TR23's first close for 2008, and before this trough. The worst day, the scenario's named day, is the window's worst return. The worst m sessions are the worst compound return over m consecutive sessions ending in (peak, trough].

A span of m sessions is dated from the close before its first return, and it may start before the peak. Exhibit 2 gives the five windows.

Example 1The 2025 window

Find the 2025 window, its worst day and its worst 5 and 20 sessions, and compare LM1's running peak.

Solution

TR23 peaked on 21 February 2025 and bottomed 30 sessions later, on 9 April, 26.54% lower. Its worst day, 7 April, lost 9.70% (LM26), five calendar days after 2 April. The worst 5 sessions, from the close of 31 March, lose 15.96%, and the worst 20, from the close of 10 March, 22.38%. Of 5,845 overlapping five-session spans, only one, ending 19 March 2020, lost more.

LM1's drawdown DDt​ measures from the running peak, 11 July 2024: 27.72% on 9 April 2025, 176 sessions later, and 18.31% on 5 August 2024. The window rule separates the two scenarios.

PitfallA "one-day" return can span ten calendar days

7 April 2025 followed four days without trading, and 30 January 2020 the Lunar New Year closure of 21 to 29 January (TWSE's 2020 schedule). Of TR23's 5,849 returns, 140 span four or more calendar days. State the span with every named day.

導讀壓力測試：先把情境的日期定清楚

壓力測試（stress testing）問的是「如果某個情境發生，這個投資組合會虧多少」，不問這個情境發生的機率。情境分析（scenario analysis）把一個情境寫成每檔基金的報酬向量，損失就是曝險（exposure）乘上報酬再加總。歷史情境（historical scenario）取自過去的崩跌，但要先用規則定好情境區間（scenario window）：高點、低點、最差的一個交易日、最差的連續 5 個與 20 個交易日，規則寫死之後才算數字。區間的高點不一定是 LM1 的回撤（drawdown）所用的歷史高點：2024 年 8 月與 2025 年 4 月共用同一個歷史高點，靠區間規則才分成兩個情境。報告「一天」的損失時，也要寫出它跨了幾個日曆日。

TR23's worst day in the 2020 window, 30 January 2020, lost 5.75%, the 8th worst of 5,849 returns, and followed the session of 20 January. Is it one day of news, and what should it be compared with?

Answer

3Replaying the Rules

replay a historical scenario: define its window, split the frozen and named-day losses by fund, and compare the rule's own path

A frozen replay holds the book of the window's first close fixed. Each fund's scenario return is its compound return over the window, and the loss splits exactly by fund:

ΔW=W0​f⊤Rsc=W0​i∑​fi​Risc​,Risc​=t∏​(1+Ri,t​)−1.​(35.1)

Here fi​Risc​ is fund i's attribution, and interest is excluded. A named-day replay applies the book of the previous close to the named day's returns, on which no rule trades. A rule's actual path is its wealth ratio from peak to trough, trades and costs included. R1 to R7 hold no cash, so on 7 April 2025 their wealth fell by exactly f⊤R (Exhibit 3, Exhibit 4).

Example 2R1 on 7 April 2025

Replay R1's book of the 2 April close on 7 April, and split the loss by fund.

Solution

After its trade of 1 April, R1's exposures on 2 April are near 0.2. With Exhibit 1's returns it loses 7.70%: −2.00 points from 0050, −2.53 from 00646, +0.36 from 00679B, −0.62 from 00635U and −2.91 from 00642U. R3, long-only minimum variance, loses 5.40%, 4.07 points from 00646. Equal weights are not equal risk on a crash day.

Example 3Frozen against actual

Compare R8's frozen and actual losses in the 2020 and 2025 windows.

Solution

Frozen at 14 January 2020, R8 loses 17.04%, but its path lost 12.58%. Its trades of 3 February and 2 March cut its exposure from 1.307 to 0.991, then to LM31's 0.884. Frozen over the whole window, those books lose 13.20% and 11.68%.

In 2025 the order reverses: 10.15% frozen at exposure 1.005, 13.84% on the path. The trade of 3 March raised the exposure to 1.319, a book that loses 13.35% over the window. The trade of 1 April took it only to 1.33 (LM31), a book that loses 13.42%.

So the 3 March trade added the leverage, and the cap of 1.5 bound at neither trade. By (31.7), a ratio of 1.319 means that the EWMA forecast lay below the window volatility of R6's book.

PitfallA frozen book ignores the rule's own trades

Report both the frozen and the actual loss, and name the trade that moved the book.

Knowledge Check 2

R6's book at the close of 21 February 2025 loses 10.10% frozen over the 2025 window, and R8's book there is R6's scaled by 1.005. (a) What is R8's frozen loss? (b) R8's path lost 13.84%. Which trade explains the gap?

Answer

(a) Scaling a book scales its scenario return, so the loss is 1.005×10.10%=10.15%. (b) The trade of 3 March raised the exposure to 1.319 (Example 3). A replay frozen at the first close misses leverage added inside the window.

導讀代理資產：向美國的基金借歷史

2008 年時 E5 的四檔海外基金都還沒上市，只能用追蹤類似資產的美國 ETF 當代理資產（proxy）。美國的收盤價先乘上之後第一筆美元兌新臺幣匯率（exchange rate），再對齊臺灣的交易日：臺灣某一天的報酬，累積了上一個臺灣交易日之後所有的美國交易日。兩個臺灣交易日之間若沒有美國交易日，代理資產的報酬就是 0，真正的基金卻可能在動。代理資產與基金的日報酬相關係數（correlation coefficient）只有 0.655 到 0.783，週報酬高得多，所以代理的誤差本身就是情境的一部分。

5Hypothetical Shocks

derive a factor shock through betas and a partial scenario from the conditional mean (16.8), and check a correlation break for positive semidefiniteness

A factor shock x to TR23 moves fund i by βi​x, where the beta βi​ is its OLS slope on TR23, LM23's (23.1). The betas are in-sample and printed without standard errors: a factor shock is a construction, and its error on a crash day is the model's.

A partial scenario fixes one fund, Ri​=x, and sets each other fund at its mean given that value. For a normal vector, LM16's (16.8) gives, fund by fund,

E[Rj​∣Ri​=x]=μj​+σii​σij​​(x−μi​).(35.3)

For other laws the right side of (35.3) is the best linear predictor (Deep Dive), here with Rˉ and Σ^.

Deep DiveDeep Dive: conditional means without normalityoptional · click to expand

Let Ri​, Rj​ have finite variances. The η and ζ that minimize E[(Rj​−ηRi​−ζ)2] are η=σij​/σii​ and ζ=μj​−ημi​, the population form of LM23's OLS. So the right side of (35.3) is the best linear predictor of Rj​ for any such law, and normality makes it the conditional mean.

For an elliptical law whose density falls with d, these values give the most plausible scenario that fixes Ri​ (Breuer et al. 2009, §2.2, p. 210, Proposition 1). They compare Kupiec's (1998) options, as they describe them.

Example 6The beta model on 7 April 2025

Apply the betas to TR23's −9.70% of 7 April 2025, and compare the partial scenario with 0050 at −10%.

Solution

On the 2,362 joint dates 0050's beta is 1.048 (R-squared 0.924) and 00646's 0.527 (0.363). Times −9.70%, the betas give Exhibit 1's last column: 00642U −1.92% against −14.62% actual, and R1 −3.62% against −7.70%. With 0050 at −10%, (35.3) moves 00646 by −4.79%, 00679B +0.71%, 00635U −1.46% and 00642U −1.69%.

Both shocks are averages of ordinary days. By LM27's split (27.8), almost all of 00642U's variance is residual (R-squared under 1%), which a factor shock leaves at its mean. On the current books a shock of −10% to TR23 costs R1 3.77% and R4 7.28%, from the unrounded betas and books.

A correlation break changes one correlation of C and keeps the rest. The new C needs λN​≥0, or no return vector has it (LM16's (16.4)).

Example 7A correlation break

Raise the correlation of 0050 and 00679B from −0.105 (LM27) to a constructed +0.30, and redo the partial scenario.

Solution

The changed C has λN​=0.298, so it stays positive definite. 00679B's conditional move turns from +0.71% to −2.04%. On the current books R3's loss rises from 1.41% to 2.95%, and R7's from 1.40% to 3.00%.

2022, % TR23 0050 00679B R2, frozen R4, frozen
Return −28.47 −33.93 −22.15 −22.37 −29.86

Yet 0050 and 00679B correlated −0.138 daily and +0.019 over 38 ISO weeks (first and last dropped), against −0.022 over the full sample's 498 (LM27). A correlation measures moves around each mean, so two funds can fall together uncorrelated.

On the 48 days when 0050 fell two standard deviations below its mean or more, it correlated 0.540 with 00646, against 0.599 overall and 0.669 by LM16's (16.10). A one-sided tail narrows a normal spread, but 0050's variance on those days (divisor 48) is 1.449 times the full sample's. So (16.10) lifts the correlation, and the data lie 1.3 normal-theory standard errors (about 0.10) below it: a direction, not a proven difference.

導讀假設情境：因子衝擊低估了崩盤日

因子模型（factor model）把基金報酬寫成 beta 乘上市場報酬，再加上與市場無關的殘差（residual）。因子衝擊（factor shock）只移動市場、殘差停在平均數（mean），所以 2025 年 4 月 7 日它算出 R1 只虧 3.62%，實際卻虧 7.70%：00642U 的 R-squared 不到 1%，它的大跌幾乎全在殘差裡。部分情境（partial scenario）用 (35.3)：固定一檔基金的報酬，其他基金取對它做迴歸（regression）的預測值；報酬為常態時，這個值就是條件期望值（conditional expectation）。改動一個相關係數（correlation coefficient）之後，要先檢查相關矩陣（correlation matrix）仍是半正定（positive semidefinite），否則它不是任何報酬的相關矩陣。

Knowledge Check 4

Under the break, R4's loss stays 6.89%. Why?

Answer

The break changes only 00679B's conditional move, and R4's current book holds no 00679B (0.000).

6Reverse Stress Testing

derive the reverse stress test, the shock of least Mahalanobis distance reaching a loss, and solve it under a price limit in cvxpy, checked by KKT
explain why a stress test assigns no probability and why surviving the named scenarios does not cover unseen losses

A reverse stress test asks for the least unusual shock that produces a given loss. LM16's squared Mahalanobis distance measures how unusual a shock x is,

d2=(x−μ)⊤Σ−1(x−μ),

and the test minimizes it subject to f⊤x=−ℓ.

TheoremThe reverse stress test

Let Σ be positive definite and f=0, with σp2​=f⊤Σf. Suppose that the loss lies beyond the book's mean, ℓ+f⊤μ>0. Then d2 has exactly one minimizer on the hyperplane f⊤x=−ℓ, and the same one on the half-space f⊤x≤−ℓ:

Rsc∗=μ−(ℓ+f⊤μ)σp2​Σf​,d∗=σp​ℓ+f⊤μ​.​(35.4)
Within d≤k the largest loss is kσp​−f⊤μ.

Proof. With z=x−μ and b=−(ℓ+f⊤μ)<0, minimize z⊤Σ−1z subject to f⊤z=b. LM28's (28.1), with Σ−1 for Σ and the single row K=f⊤, gives z∗=bΣf/σp2​ and d∗2=b2/σp2​. On the half-space, scaling z by b/f⊤z∈(0,1] reaches the hyperplane without raising d2. For the bound, u⊤Σ−1v is an inner product because Σ−1 is positive definite, and Cauchy–Schwarz in it gives −f⊤z≤dσp​, with equality at z=−kΣf/σp​. ■

The bound is Breuer et al.'s worst loss over the ellipsoid d≤k (2009, §2.3, p. 211), who measure plausibility by d (§2.1, p. 209). This book ranks shocks by d without a probability, since LM16's law of d2 behind (16.9) holds only for normal returns. The shocks are plug-in estimates with an unmeasured sampling error.

Deep DiveDeep Dive: the reverse stress test with a price limitoptional · click to expand

The bounded test minimizes d2 over {x:f⊤x≤−ℓ, x0050​≥−10%}, a non-empty polyhedron because every book holds a fund besides 0050. The objective is strictly convex and grows without bound, so exactly one minimizer exists. With linear constraints, LM29's conditions (29.4) are necessary and sufficient.

With both constraints active, Kx=b for the rows f⊤ and (1,0,0,0,0) of K and b=(−ℓ,−10%)⊤. This K has full row rank, since f is no multiple of (1,0,0,0,0). Then (28.1), with Σ^−1 for Σ and the shock measured from Rˉ, gives

x∗=Rˉ+Σ^K⊤(KΣ^K⊤)−1(b−KRˉ).
By (29.4) this point is the answer when both multipliers ν1​, ν2​ are non-negative: for R1 they are 37.3 and 0.8877 in percent units. For R8 the limit is inactive, and (35.4) holds. CLARABEL and OSQP agree with the closed form to 10−9, with stationarity residuals below 10−13 and complementary slackness below 10−9.

The widget puts any shock on any current book. It starts at R1 under 7 April 2025's returns: a loss of 7.78%, ruin from leverage 12.85 (Section 8), distance 12.94 and 2 of 2,362 days farther.

PitfallThe closest shock can be impossible in one day

R1's shock puts 0050 at −10.47%, beyond its daily limit. Bound the shock by the limit, and compare each entry with the fund's worst day.

Python
import cvxpy as cp
import numpy as np

# Rbar, S: mean and covariance of the 2,362 joint dates (divisor T), in %;
# f: a book's exposures, 0050 first; loss = 10 (%)
sp2 = f @ S @ f
x_closed = Rbar - (loss + f @ Rbar) * (S @ f) / sp2      # (35.4)
d_closed = (loss + f @ Rbar) / np.sqrt(sp2)

Si = np.linalg.inv(S)
Si = (Si + Si.T) / 2                                     # exactly symmetric
x = cp.Variable(len(f))
cons = [f @ x <= -loss, x[0] >= -10.0]                   # 0050's limit
d2 = cp.quad_form(x - Rbar, cp.psd_wrap(Si))
prob = cp.Problem(cp.Minimize(d2), cons)
prob.solve(solver=cp.CLARABEL, tol_gap_abs=1e-12, tol_gap_rel=1e-12,
           tol_feas=1e-12)
assert prob.status == "optimal" and x.value is not None
nu = [float(c.dual_value) for c in cons]                 # both >= 0
grad = 2 * Si @ (x.value - Rbar) + nu[0] * f - nu[1] * np.eye(len(f))[0]
print(np.sqrt(d2.value), np.abs(grad).max())             # KKT residual
導讀反向壓力測試：從損失倒推最不離譜的衝擊

一般的壓力測試（stress testing）先給情境再算損失；反向壓力測試（reverse stress test）先定損失，例如一天虧 10%，再從所有造成這個損失的衝擊（shock）中找出最不離譜的一個。「離譜」用 Mahalanobis 距離（Mahalanobis distance）衡量，它同時考慮各基金的波動與相關，大漲和大跌一樣算不尋常。答案有公式解（closed form）(35.4)：距離等於損失加上平均報酬（mean return），再除以投資組合（portfolio）的標準差（standard deviation）。最近的衝擊可能讓 0050 跌超過 10% 的漲跌幅限制（price limit），加上限制後用 cvxpy 求解，再以 KKT 條件（KKT conditions）檢查。距離只用來排序，不是機率。

7Limits, Locks and Overnight Gaps

explain how price limits, holidays and overnight gaps change a "one-day" loss, and which funds have no daily limit under TWSE's and TPEx's rules

TWSE's 受益憑證買賣辦法 (Rules Governing Trading of Beneficial Certificates), Art. 8 ¶1, gives a listed fund the stock limit of 10%. It exempts index ETFs with foreign constituents, such as 00646, and futures-trust ETFs on a foreign futures index, such as 00635U and 00642U, items standing since 3 June 2016.

TPEx's ETF rules, Art. 7 ¶1 item 一, exempt index ETFs with foreign constituents, and TPEx's listing notice gave 00679B no limit from its first day (Exhibit 6).

For 2008 and 2015 the proxies' returns carry no daily limit, since their funds have none under today's rules, and 0050's returns carry the limit of their day (Exhibit 2).

A limit-down lock is a session that trades only at the limit-down price: open, high, low and close are equal. Sales execute at the limit or not at all, and an unfilled sale waits for the next open (Example 9).

A close also hides when the loss arrived. Exhibit 7 gives the TAIEX price index's log changes from the previous close to 09:01:00, to the day's low and to the close. On 7 April 2025 the whole fall came in the first minute. LM39 models such jumps, which this module replays without a probability.

8Leverage, Ruin and Margin

compute a levered book's loss at a limit-down lock and at the next open, its ruin leverage −1/R, and the fall that triggers a margin call
compare weak growth, a drawdown limit, funding and ruin as four failures of the same rules

A unit book with scenario return Rpsc​<0, scaled to total exposure L with holdings fixed, ends at 1+LRpsc​ times its wealth. So it is ruined, equity at or below zero, when

1+LRpsc​≤0,that isL≥Lruin​=−Rpsc​1​.​(35.2)

Over a window the test applies on every date, so Rpsc​ is then the book's lowest value. R6's frozen books reach it at each window's last close. R3's and R5's 2024 books reach it on 26 July, at −1.40% and −1.47%, and end at +0.11% and −0.07%. Ruin is reported with its date, never floored (LM27).

Exhibit 8 draws (35.2) for R6's book in three replays and for 0050 to the open of 8 April 2025. The lines cross zero at 7.67, 9.90, 16.77 and 7.10. A book of 0050 levered five times lost 50.00% at the lock and 70.45% by the next open. R8's cap, 1.5, and its largest uncapped ratio, 2.14 (LM31), lie far to the left.

Example 10One TX contract on its initial margin

Hold one TX contract at Ft​=49,357, NT$200 a point, with TAIFEX's initial margin as the only equity. Find f, the fall that reaches the maintenance margin, and the equity after a 10% limit day.

Solution

The contract is worth NT$9,871,400 (LM23). TAIFEX's table effective 12 August 2026 sets the initial margin at NT$701,000 and the maintenance margin at NT$538,000 (LM2). So f=9,871,400/701,000=14.08, and a fall of 163,000/9,871,400=1.65% reaches the maintenance margin. A 10% limit day loses NT$987,140, 1.41 times the equity, and leaves NT$−286,140: ruin, a loss beyond the equity, reported and not floored.

A margin call is a funding failure, distinct from ruin. It comes long before equity reaches zero, and on a locked day the position may not close in time. How exchanges set margins, and how futures and leveraged funds carry leverage, is LM46's subject. Rules that cut exposure as a drawdown deepens are LM44's drawdown control. Here exposure stays fixed through the scenario.

導讀槓桿、權益歸零與保證金追繳

槓桿（leverage）把情境報酬放大 L 倍：部位不變時，當 1+LRpsc​≤0 就權益歸零（ruin: zero equity），臨界的曝險（exposure）是 −1/Rpsc​。從 2025 年 4 月 2 日收盤到 4 月 8 日開盤，0050 跌了 14.09%，槓桿 7.10 倍以上的 0050 部位就權益歸零。期貨的槓桿更高：只放原始保證金（initial margin）時，一口台指期是 14.08 倍，下跌 1.65% 就碰到維持保證金（maintenance margin），面臨保證金追繳（margin call）；一根 10% 的跌停會虧掉 1.41 倍的權益。保證金追繳是資金面的失敗，通常比權益歸零早得多，市場鎖住時部位甚至出不了場。

9What a Stress Test Cannot Say

explain why a stress test assigns no probability and why surviving the named scenarios does not cover unseen losses
compare weak growth, a drawdown limit, funding and ruin as four failures of the same rules

A stress test assigns no probability: its replays are a few past paths, and its constructed shocks are chosen, not drawn. The Basel Committee asks for "severe but plausible" scenarios, and describes reverse stress tests as exploring what could make a bank fail (BCBS 2018, Principle 4, p. 6).

The same rules can fail in four ways (Exhibit 9). Weak growth shows in LM34's net log growth, a drawdown limit meets LM32's MDD or DDt0​ (32.9), and funding and ruin are this module's. Growth and drawdowns come from one realized path, while ruin stays far away in every replay.

PitfallSurviving the scenarios does not cover unseen losses

A book that survives every replay has survived five past paths and a few constructions, not every loss. Filtered historical simulation, scenarios generated by empirical likelihood and stress tests of liquidity extend the method beyond this book.

Summary

  • A scenario's P&L, W0​f⊤Rsc (35.1), splits exactly by fund.
  • Windows are fixed by rule, and a named day can span ten calendar days.
  • Frozen, named-day and actual replays differ: R8's own trades raised its 2025 loss.
  • Proxies pair a New York close with the next exchange rate, (35.5), and their error is part of the scenario.
  • Factor shocks and partial scenarios (35.3) average ordinary days. A correlation break must stay positive semidefinite.
  • The reverse stress test (35.4) finds the least unusual shock reaching a loss, ranked by a distance, not a probability.
  • A lock moves a sale to the next open. Ruin starts at Lruin​=−1/Rpsc​ (35.2), and a margin call comes earlier.
  • Weak growth, drawdowns, funding and ruin are four failures of the same rules, and surviving the replays does not cover unseen losses.
