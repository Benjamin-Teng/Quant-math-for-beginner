Eigen-decomposition and PCA

The directions in which a covariance matrix only stretches: how many separate movements 28 Taiwan sectors have, how stable they are, and why the inverse matrix amplifies error.

Learning Module Overview
  • An eigenvector of M is a direction that M only stretches, Mx=λk​x. The daily covariance matrix of Semiconductor and FinancialInsurance stretches the direction 34.2° from the Semiconductor axis by 3.0492 and the perpendicular one by 0.7559, in units of 10−4.
  • A real symmetric matrix has real eigenvalues and orthonormal eigenvectors, so Σ^=∑k​λk​qk​qk⊤​. Each eigenvalue is the variance in the direction of its eigenvector, and every unit direction has a variance between λN​ and λ1​.
  • The first principal component maximizes variance over unit vectors, and its Lagrange multiplier is the eigenvalue. Over 2008–2026 one direction carries 52.6% of the standardized variance of 28 Taiwan sector indices, but 12 directions are needed to pass 80%.
  • Covariance PCA follows the most volatile sectors and changes with units; correlation PCA treats every sector alike. Restating Semiconductor in percent turns its covariance PC1 into Semiconductor alone, with a share of 99.996%.
  • An aggregate index beside its members creates a near-zero eigenvalue: the correlation matrix of Electronic and its eight sub-industries has λ9​=0.0073 and condition number 836.
  • A perturbation rotates eigenvectors by about its size divided by the eigenvalue gap. The first component barely moves between years (cosine at least 0.98); beyond it, the components of the two halves of the sample change places (second cosine 0.297).
  • The inverse weights each direction by 1/λk​. Between the two halves U28's covariance matrix moved by 24.5% in the spectral norm and the solution of Σ^x=1 by 0.729 of the second half's solution length, against a worst case of 30.93.

1Introduction

A covariance matrix of 28 sectors has 406 distinct entries (LM10). Its structure lies in the perpendicular directions that it only stretches, its eigenvectors, and in the stretch factors, its eigenvalues. They rank the data's uncorrelated movements by variance and explain why the inverse matrix magnifies small changes.

Motivating CaseTwenty-eight sectors, how many movements?

Take U28, the 28 leaf indices among the 30 Taiwan Stock Exchange sector indices that FinMind serves daily: all but the aggregates Electronic and ChemicalBiotechnologyMedicalCare. From 2 December 2008 to 8 October 2026 their daily returns are all positively correlated, from 0.217 (BiotechnologyMedicalCare and OilGasElectricity) to 0.755 (ComputerPeripheralEquipment and ElectronicPartsComponents).

One fixed mix of the 28 standardized returns, with entries from 0.130 to 0.224, carries 52.6% of their total variance. Yet 12 such directions are needed to pass 80%, and the first one's share was 66.6% in 2020 but 34.5% in 2017. A second direction is positive on the eight electronics sub-industries and on BiotechnologyMedicalCare, Chemical and ElectricMachinery, and negative on the other 17.

On 7 April 2025 all 28 fell, by 5.90% (CommunicationsInternet) to 9.95% (Semiconductor). Yet over the sample all 28 moved the same way on only 300 of 4,382 days (6.8%).

Solved on each half of the sample, the linear system Σ^x=1 moves Semiconductor's entry from 430.84 to −587.60 and FinancialInsurance's from −2,547.30 to 2,216.20 (Exhibit 1). How many separate movements are there, do they hold over time, and why does the solution move so much more than the matrix? Sections 5, 7 and 8 answer.

Sections 2–4 build the tools: eigenvectors, the spectral theorem and the variance in any direction. Sections 5–7 turn them into principal component analysis and test its stability. Section 8 explains the moving solution, and Section 9 does the work in Python.

NoteResearch hygiene

The universe (U28) and the sample were fixed before any result was computed. Full-sample eigenvalues, shares, loadings and condition numbers are descriptive: they summarize the past, and nobody inside the sample could have known them. Comparisons across calendar years and across the two halves, split by time, are a stability check, not an out-of-sample test. A low cosine between two periods cannot tell noise from a real change.

2Directions a Matrix Only Stretches

calculate the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a 2×2 symmetric matrix by hand, and of an N×N one with eigh

Write M:=104Σ^ for the daily returns of Semiconductor and FinancialInsurance on the U28 sample:

M=(2.32341.0666​1.06661.4817​).

Multiply every unit vector u by M. The unit circle becomes an ellipse (Exhibit 2), and most vectors also turn: e1​ goes to (2.3234,1.0666)⊤, which points 24.7° away. Two perpendicular directions do not turn. Along them M only stretches, by 3.0492 along one and by 0.7559 along the other.

DefinitionEigenvalue and eigenvector

Let M be an N×N matrix. A number λk​ and a vector x=0 with Mx=λk​x are an eigenvalue and an eigenvector of M, together an eigenpair. The eigenvector keeps its direction under M and is stretched by the factor λk​, reversed if λk​<0.

Rewrite the definition as (M−λk​I)x=0. A non-zero solution exists exactly when M−λk​I is singular (LM9, (9.6)), so

⟺ ​Mx=λk​x  for some x=0det(M−λk​I)=0.​(11.1)

The second line is the characteristic equation. For a 2×2 matrix and an unknown ℓ it is a quadratic, with the trace trM=m11​+m22​:

0​=det(M−ℓI)=(m11​−ℓ)(m22​−ℓ)−m12​m21​=ℓ2−(trM)ℓ+detM.​

The quadratic formula gives both roots:

λ1,2​=21​trM±41​(trM)2−detM​.(11.2)

Factored, the quadratic is (ℓ−λ1​)(ℓ−λ2​). So the eigenvalues add up to the trace and multiply to the determinant: λ1​+λ2​=trM and λ1​λ2​=detM. Section 3 extends both facts to symmetric matrices of any size.

A rotation by an angle other than 0° or 180° turns every vector of the plane, so it has no real eigenvector (Strang 2016, §6.1, Example 5). Symmetry excludes such matrices.

Example 1The eigenpairs of a two-sector covariance matrix

Find the eigenvalues and unit eigenvectors of M=104Σ^ for Semiconductor and FinancialInsurance, with m11​=2.3234, m22​=1.4817 and m12​=1.0666.

Solution

The trace is 3.8051, so 21​trM=1.90255. For a symmetric matrix the term under the root of (11.2) equals 41​(m11​−m22​)2+m122​ (Section 3), here 0.17711+1.13764=1.31475, with root 1.14663. So λ1​=3.0492 and λ2​=0.7559. They add up to the trace, and their product 2.3049 is the determinant 2.3234×1.4817−1.06662.

For λ1​, the first row of (M−λ1​I)x=0 reads −0.7258x1​+1.0666x2​=0. So x2​/x1​=0.6805, an angle of 34.2° from the Semiconductor axis, and the unit eigenvector is q1​=(0.827,0.563)⊤. The second is perpendicular to it (Section 3), q2​=(−0.563,0.827)⊤.

PitfallAn eigenvalue is not a sector's variance

Here λ1​=3.0492 exceeds both variances, 2.3234 and 1.4817, and λ2​=0.7559 is below both. Each eigenvalue is the variance of a mix along its eigenvector (Section 4). The mix 0.827 Semiconductor plus 0.563 FinancialInsurance has 1.31 times the variance of Semiconductor alone.

Knowledge Check 1

A symmetric 2×2 matrix has trace 5 and determinant 4. Find its eigenvalues without taking a square root.

Answer

The eigenvalues add up to 5 and multiply to 4, so they are 4 and 1, the roots of ℓ2−5ℓ+4=0.

3Symmetric Matrices: the Spectral Theorem

explain the spectral theorem and its conditions, and write a covariance matrix as Σ^=QΛQ⊤=∑k​λk​qk​qk⊤​

Covariance and correlation matrices are symmetric, mij​=mji​, and symmetry buys three properties.

TheoremSpectral theorem

Let M be a real symmetric N×N matrix. Then (i) all its eigenvalues are real; (ii) eigenvectors of different eigenvalues are orthogonal; (iii) one can choose mutually orthogonal unit eigenvectors q1​,…,qN​, so that Q⊤Q=I and

M=QΛQ⊤=k=1∑N​λk​qk​qk⊤​.(11.3)

Proof for N=2. (i) The term under the root of (11.2) is

​41​(m11​+m22​)2−(m11​m22​−m122​)=41​(m11​−m22​)2+m122​ ≥0,​

so both roots are real. They are equal only when m11​=m22​ and m12​=0, that is, when M is a multiple of I.

(ii) Let Mx1​=λ1​x1​ and Mx2​=λ2​x2​ with λ1​=λ2​, and read x1⊤​Mx2​ two ways. Multiplying Mx2​ first gives λ2​x1⊤​x2​. By symmetry x1⊤​M=(Mx1​)⊤, which gives λ1​x1⊤​x2​. So (λ1​−λ2​)x1⊤​x2​=0 and x1⊤​x2​=0, for any N.

(iii) Scale the eigenvectors to unit length; if λ1​=λ2​, M is a multiple of I and any orthonormal pair will do. The columns of MQ are λ1​q1​ and λ2​q2​, so MQ=QΛ. Orthonormal columns give Q⊤Q=I, hence Q−1=Q⊤ and M=QΛQ⊤. Written as columns times rows, as in LM10's (10.2), this product is the sum in (11.3). ■

Section 5 proves (i) and (iii) for every N. Three consequences follow from (11.3).

Trace and determinant. The diagonal of qk​qk⊤​ sums to qk⊤​qk​=1, so trM=∑k​λk​. Since Q−1=Q⊤, also QQ⊤=I, and the product rule (9.3) gives detM=∏k​λk​.

Definiteness. In the coordinates y=Q⊤x, (11.3) gives x⊤Mx=∑k​λk​yk2​. So M is positive semidefinite exactly when every λk​≥0 (take x=qk​ for the converse), and positive definite exactly when every λk​>0. This is LM10's promised eigenvalue test (Strang 2016, §6.5), and a sample covariance matrix, positive semidefinite by (10.5), never has a negative eigenvalue.

Signs and ties. If qk​ is a unit eigenvector, so is −qk​. The book's sign rule makes the entry largest in absolute value positive, the first of them on a tie. A 2×2 correlation matrix with ρ>0 thus has q2​=(1,−1)⊤/2​; for ρ<0 the same vector is q1​. When two eigenvalues are equal, every unit vector in the plane of their eigenvectors is an eigenvector. Orthonormal eigenvectors can then be chosen, but only the plane, the eigenspace, is determined.

LM7 used the annualized covariance matrix Σ=AΣ^ of 0050 and 00679B over 2017–2026, with A=243.0543 on its sample. Its entries are σ12​=0.040425, σ22​=0.018214 and σ12​=−0.002866, and LM7 quoted its eigenvalues without proof. Find the eigenpairs and the volatilities along the two axes.

Solution

The trace is 0.058639, and (11.2) gives λ1​=0.040789 and λ2​=0.017850. The first row of (Σ−λ1​I)x=0 gives x2​/x1​=(0.040789−0.040425)/(−0.002866)=−0.1270, so q1​=(0.992,−0.126)⊤, tilted −7.24∘ from the 0050 axis. The axis volatilities, λ1​​=20.20% and λ2​​=13.36%, are close to the funds' 20.11% and 13.50%: a correlation of −0.106 tilts the axes only slightly (Exhibit 3).

PitfallComparing eigenvectors entry by entry

An eigenvector's sign is a convention, and when two eigenvalues are close the vector itself can turn. Compare two periods' eigenvectors by ∣cosθ∣, the absolute value of their dot product (Section 7), never entry by entry.

導讀對稱矩陣：沿互相垂直的方向拉伸

矩陣把單位圓變成橢圓。大多數向量（vector）會被轉向，只有特徵向量（eigenvector）的方向不變，只被拉長或壓短，倍數就是特徵值（eigenvalue）。共變異數矩陣（covariance matrix）是對稱（symmetric）的，所以譜定理（spectral theorem）保證特徵值都是實數、特徵向量可以取成互相垂直，矩陣可以寫成幾個秩一（rank-one）矩陣的加總，這就是譜分解（spectral decomposition）。特徵值的和等於各變數變異數（variance）的總和；三個產業的例子中，第一個方向就占了 70.0%。特徵向量的正負號沒有意義，本書規定絕對值最大的元素為正；兩個特徵值相等時，只有它們張成的平面是確定的。

4Variance in a Direction: the Rayleigh Quotient and the Condition Number

derive the first principal component as the unit vector of largest variance, with the eigenvalue as its Lagrange multiplier
calculate the condition number κ=λ1​/λN​ and explain why solving a system with Σ^ amplifies estimation error

Read u⊤Rt​, for a unit vector u, as a mix of the N returns, rebalanced daily. Its sample variance is u⊤Σ^u (LM10, (10.4)), the variance of the data in the direction u. For any symmetric M and any u=0, the ratio

h(u):=u⊤uu⊤Mu​

is the Rayleigh quotient. Rescaling u does not change it, so unit vectors suffice.

PropositionBounds on the variance in a direction

Let M be symmetric with eigenvalues λ1​≥⋯≥λN​. For every unit vector u,

λN​≤u⊤Mu≤λ1​,(11.4)
with equality on the right at u=q1​ and on the left at u=qN​.

Proof. Put y=Q⊤u. Since QQ⊤=I, ∥y∥2=u⊤QQ⊤u=1. By (11.3), u⊤Mu=y⊤Λy=∑k​λk​yk2​: an average of the eigenvalues with weights yk2​ that sum to 1. It lies between the smallest and the largest eigenvalue, and u=q1​ puts all the weight on λ1​. ■

LM7 used these bounds without proof: its smallest and largest curvatures of Σ are λ2​ and λ1​ of Example 2. Exhibit 4 traces h(u) for Semiconductor and FinancialInsurance.

The condition number. For a positive definite M, the condition number is κ:=λ1​/λN​, the largest variance in any direction over the smallest. It is 1 for a multiple of I and grows as the ellipse of Exhibit 2 flattens; a singular M has an infinite κ.

A 2×2 correlation matrix has eigenvalues 1+ρ and 1−ρ, with eigenvectors (1,1)⊤/2​ and (1,−1)⊤/2​, so

κ(C)=1−∣ρ∣1+∣ρ∣​.

This condition number measures LM7's warning about correlations near ±1. On LM8's sample, 0050 and 00631L have ρ=0.974365, so κ(C)=77.0: a correlation that looks merely high makes the variance in one direction 77 times that in another (Exhibit 5).

Example 4Equal correlations

Let N assets have the same correlation ρ>0, so C=(1−ρ)I+ρ11⊤. Find its eigenvalues and κ. Then use u=1/N​ in (11.4) to bound λ1​ of U28's correlation matrix, whose 756 off-diagonal entries average ρˉ​=0.50227.

Solution

Since 1⊤1=N, C1=(1+(N−1)ρ)1, so 1+(N−1)ρ is an eigenvalue. Every x in the space of dimension N−1 orthogonal to 1 has Cx=(1−ρ)x, so 1−ρ is an eigenvalue N−1 times, and κ=(1+(N−1)ρ)/(1−ρ).

For U28, h(1/N​)=1⊤C1/N=1+27×0.50227=14.561, which by (11.4) is at most λ1​; indeed λ1​=14.721.

Deep DiveWhy LM7's gradient ascent contracts by (κ − 1)/(κ + 1)optional · click to expand

In LM7's notation, f are exposures, g is the quadratic growth model with Hessian −Σ and optimum f∗, and α is the step. Since ∇g(f)=−Σ(f−f∗), a step to f+α∇g(f) turns the error f−f∗ into (I−αΣ)(f−f∗), which multiplies its component along qk​ by 1−αλk​. With α=2/(λ1​+λN​) the extreme factors are ±(κ−1)/(κ+1) and the others are smaller in size, so each step shrinks the distance to f∗ at least by that factor. Example 2's eigenvalues give (0.040789−0.017850)/(0.040789+0.017850)=0.391 at α=34.1, LM7's 0.39.

PitfallSay which matrix a condition number belongs to

Rescaling the variables changes the eigenvalues, so κ(C)=κ(Σ^): for U28 they are 70.6 and 108.1. A daily and an annualized Σ^ share one κ, because multiplying a matrix by A multiplies every eigenvalue by A.

導讀方向上的變異數與條件數

把 N 個報酬的一個固定組合看成單位向量（unit vector）u（長度為 1，不是權重和為 1 的投資組合），它的樣本變異數（sample variance）就是 u⊤Σ^u。換到特徵向量（eigenvector）座標後，這個數是各特徵值（eigenvalue）的加權平均，權重加總為 1，所以一定落在最小與最大特徵值之間，最大值就在第一個特徵向量上。最大特徵值除以最小特徵值是條件數（condition number），代表橢圓有多扁。0050 與 00631L 的相關係數（correlation coefficient）0.974 看似只是「很高」，相關矩陣（correlation matrix）的條件數卻是 77：某個方向的變異數是另一個方向的 77 倍。相關矩陣與共變異數矩陣（covariance matrix）的條件數不同，引用時要說清楚是哪一個。

5Principal Component Analysis

derive the first principal component as the unit vector of largest variance, with the eigenvalue as its Lagrange multiplier
interpret explained shares, loadings and scores, and compare covariance with correlation PCA

Principal component analysis (PCA) asks which mix of the N returns varies most, then which varies most among the mixes uncorrelated with it, and so on. A mix is a vector u; its centred return on date t is u⊤(Rt​−Rˉ), with sample variance u⊤Σ^u.

The question needs a constraint, and portfolio weights, 1⊤u=1, do not work. The vector (a,1−a,0,…,0)⊤ satisfies them for every number a, and its variance grows like a2 times the variance of R1,t​−R2,t​. PCA fixes the length instead, u⊤u=1, and compares directions.

The Lagrangian is L=u⊤Σ^u−λ(u⊤u−1), with gradient 2Σ^u−2λu by LM10's (10.8). Setting it to zero gives Σ^u=λu. So every stationary point is a unit eigenvector, and its multiplier is the eigenvalue, which is also the variance there: u⊤Σ^u=λu⊤u=λ.

A continuous function on a closed and bounded set, such as the unit sphere, attains a largest value. This extreme value theorem is stated without proof; Strang and Herman (2016, §4.7, Theorem 4.18) give the case of two variables. So the maximum is the stationary point with the largest value:

u⊤u=1max​u⊤Σ^u=λ1​,attained at u=q1​.(11.5)

Maximizing again over unit vectors orthogonal to q1​ gives q2​, with variance λ2​, and so on. The directions qk​ are the principal components, and their entries are loadings.

Deep DivePCA proves the spectral theorem for every Noptional · click to expand

Let M be real symmetric, with Lagrange multipliers λ and ζ. The maximum of u⊤Mu on the unit sphere exists, and the Lagrange condition makes it a real eigenpair (λ1​,q1​). Next maximize over unit vectors with q1⊤​u=0. The gradient of u⊤Mu−λ(u⊤u−1)−ζq1⊤​u is 2Mu−2λu−ζq1​, and its dot product with q1​ is −ζ, since q1⊤​Mu=λ1​q1⊤​u=0. So ζ=0, and the maximizer is a real eigenpair (λ2​,q2​) with q2​⊥q1​. Repeating N times gives (11.3), with no complex numbers and no characteristic polynomial.

The construction proves (iii), and (i) follows. Let Mx=ℓx for a number ℓ and a vector x=0, both possibly complex. Expanding x in the orthonormal qk​ (still valid, since QQ⊤x=x) gives ∑k​(λk​−ℓ)(qk⊤​x)qk​=0, so every coefficient is zero. Some qk⊤​x is not zero, so ℓ equals that λk​ and is real.

Shares. The eigenvalues add up to the trace, the sum of the N variances, so component k carries the explained share

trΣ^λk​​=λ1​+⋯+λN​λk​​(=Nλk​​  for C)(11.6)

of the total variance. A scree plot shows the shares against k (Exhibit 6).

Scores. The score of component k on date t in correlation PCA is

ξk,t​=qk⊤​diag(s)−1(Rt​−Rˉ),

the day's standardized returns read along qk​. Their covariance matrix is C, so Varξk​=qk⊤​Cqk​=λk​ (divisor T), and two different scores, ξk​ and ξk′​, have covariance qk′⊤​Cqk​=λk​qk′⊤​qk​=0. PCA turns N correlated series into N uncorrelated ones, ranked by variance.

Covariance or correlation. Covariance PCA decomposes Σ^, correlation PCA decomposes C. New units for one variable rescale its row and column of Σ^ but leave C alone. On U28 the two analyses agree on the first component but not on the second (Exhibit 8).

導讀主成分分析：讓資料自己選方向

主成分分析（principal component analysis）找的是變異數（variance）最大的方向：在長度為 1 的向量（vector）中求 u⊤Σ^u 的最大值。拉格朗日乘數（Lagrange multiplier）法的駐點（stationary point）一定是特徵向量（eigenvector），乘數就是特徵值（eigenvalue）。限制必須是長度 u⊤u=1，不能是權重和為 1，否則變異數可以無限大。28 個產業中，第一主成分占標準化變異數的 52.6%，負荷量（loading）全部為正，表示產業大多同漲同跌；但要 12 個主成分才超過 80%。共變異數矩陣（covariance matrix）的主成分會被波動最大的產業主導，也會隨單位改變，相關矩陣（correlation matrix）則不受單位影響。

7Eigenvalue Gaps and the Stability of Components

explain the sign convention for eigenvectors and why near-equal eigenvalues make their eigenvectors unstable
compare principal components across calendar years and halves of a sample, and label the comparison a stability check

How far does an eigenvector turn when the matrix changes a little? Rotate the axes of a symmetric 2×2 matrix by an angle θ. The unit vectors q1​=(cosθ,sinθ)⊤ and q2​=(−sinθ,cosθ)⊤ are its eigenvectors exactly when Mq1​ has no component along q2​:

q2⊤​Mq1​=​m12​cos2θ−21​(m11​−m22​)sin2θ=0.​

Start from a diagonal matrix with neighbouring eigenvalues λk​>λk+1​, and add a small ε to both off-diagonal entries. The eigenvectors turn by

θ=21​arctanλk​−λk+1​2ε​≈λk​−λk+1​ε​,fromtan2θ=m11​−m22​2m12​​.​(11.7)

The arctangent is exact; the approximation drops terms of third order in ε over the gap. The rotation is the perturbation divided by the gap, so a gap no larger than ε can turn the eigenvectors by tens of degrees, up to 45°. Equal correlations are the limit, with every gap below λ1​ zero (Example 4).

Example 8How far noise can turn a component

U28's correlation matrix has λ2​=1.48370, λ3​=1.14694, λ7​=0.58028 and λ8​=0.54445. Let noise add ε=0.05 to the entry linking two neighbouring eigenvectors. Find the rotation by (11.7), exactly and to first order.

Solution

For components 2 and 3 the gap is 0.33676, and ε over the gap is 0.1485. The exact angle is 21​arctan(0.1/0.33676)=8.27∘; to first order it is 0.1485 radians, 8.51∘.

For components 7 and 8 the gap is 0.03583, smaller than ε. The exact angle is 35.1∘, but the first-order formula gives 1.3955 radians, 80.0∘. It fails once ε is not small next to the gap, and components 7 and 8 are barely determined.

Calendar years. Each year from 2009 to 2026 (2026 to 8 October, 185 returns) gives its own correlation matrix. December 2008's 22 returns enter only the full sample and the halves. The first component's share moves from 34.5% (2017) to 66.6% (2020) (Exhibit 10). Its direction hardly moves: its cosine with the full sample's q1​ is at least 0.98 every year, and lowest in 2026, at 0.981 (Exhibit 11).

The second component's cosine runs from 0.119 (2016) to 0.946 (2025). When λ2​ and λ3​ are close, one period's q2​ can be a mix of another period's q2​ and q3​. With the first half as the reference, the second half's q2​ has the plane measure

(q2(1)⊤​q2(2)​)2+(q3(1)⊤​q2(2)​)2​.

Since q2(1)​ and q3(1)​ are orthonormal, this measure is the cosine of the angle between q2(2)​ and their plane. The measure is one-sided: it shows where the compared period's q2​ went, not that the plane holds. For a year's q2​ against the full sample's plane, it runs from 0.690 (2020) to 0.959 (2024).

Two halves. The halves hold 2,191 returns each (Exhibit 12). The first components agree almost perfectly, ∣cosθ1​∣=0.997; the second do not, ∣cosθ2​∣=0.297. PC2's largest loading is OilGasElectricity (0.572) in the first half and ElectronicPartsComponents (0.341) in the second, with a signed cosine of −0.297. The plane measure is 0.29662+0.87872​=0.9274, an angle of 22°: the second half's q2​ is mostly the first half's q3​ (cosine 0.879).

The reverse measure, the first half's q2​ against the second half's plane, is only 0.29662+0.41962​=0.514. That vector is mostly the second half's q4​ (cosine 0.776): beyond the first, the components changed places. The plane does not hold either. The plane measure of the second half's q3​ is only 0.437, an angle of 64° from the first half's plane. In that half, λ3​ and λ4​ differ by only 0.069, so (11.7) leaves q3​ poorly determined.

ResultThe case, second question

The first direction holds over time, with cosines of at least 0.98 every year and 0.997 across the halves. Only its share moves, between 34.5% and 66.6%. The second direction does not hold (0.297): the first half's third direction ranks second in the second half (0.879), and its second ranks fourth (0.776). Beyond the first direction the components change places, so only the dominant direction is worth describing.

PitfallA stability check is not an out-of-sample test

Comparing the halves shows how much the estimates move; it tests nothing. An out-of-sample test fits on earlier data and judges on later data that the fit never used (Research hygiene, LM34). An eigenvalue estimated from 250 returns also has sampling error of its own, and this module attaches no confidence intervals (LM20, LM22).

導讀特徵值間距決定主成分穩不穩

矩陣受到一點擾動（perturbation）ε 時，特徵向量（eigenvector）轉動的角度大約是 ε 除以兩個特徵值（eigenvalue）的間距；間距很小時，一點雜訊就能讓方向轉動幾十度。28 個產業中，第一個特徵值遠大於其他，所以第一主成分（principal component）在每一年、前後兩半都幾乎不動。第二個以後的特徵值小而接近：第二主成分在兩半之間的餘弦（cosine）只有 0.297，後半段的第二主成分大致是前半段的第三主成分（0.879），前半段的第二主成分則落到第四（0.776），第二、第三主成分張成的平面也不穩定。平面度量（plane measure）只看單邊，不能證明平面穩定。比較兩個期間的特徵向量要看餘弦的絕對值，不要逐一比較元素；這是穩定性檢查（stability check），不是樣本外檢定（out-of-sample test）。

8The Inverse of Σ^ and Error Amplification

calculate the condition number κ=λ1​/λN​ and explain why solving a system with Σ^ amplifies estimation error

Rescaled to sum to 1, the solution of Σ^x=1 is the minimum-variance portfolio of LM28; here it is only a linear system. When every λk​>0, the inverse of QΛQ⊤ is QΛ−1Q⊤, because their product is QQ⊤=I. Written as a sum,

Σ^−1b=k=1∑N​λk​qk⊤​b​qk​.(11.8)

Each direction enters with the weight 1/λk​. The smallest eigenvalues get the largest weights. In U28 they also lie close together, so by (11.7) their eigenvectors are poorly determined. Between the halves' covariance matrices, 12 of the 14 eigenvectors of the smallest eigenvalues have ∣cosθ∣ below 0.25.

The spectral norm. The spectral norm ∥M∥2​ is the largest length of Mu over unit vectors u. For symmetric M, ∥Mu∥2=u⊤M2u and M2=QΛ2Q⊤, so by (11.4) ∥M∥2​ is the largest ∣λk​∣. A positive definite M thus has ∥M∥2​=λ1​, ∥M−1∥2​=1/λN​ and κ=∥M∥2​∥M−1∥2​.

PropositionHow far a solution can move

Let M be positive definite with Mx=b, and let a change ΔM move the solution to x+Δx, so that (M+ΔM)(x+Δx)=b. Then

∥x+Δx∥∥Δx∥​≤κ∥M∥2​∥ΔM∥2​​.(11.9)

Proof. Subtracting Mx=b leaves MΔx=−ΔM(x+Δx), so Δx=−M−1ΔM(x+Δx). Since ∥Ky∥≤∥K∥2​∥y∥ for any matrix K and vector y, ∥Δx∥≤∥M−1∥2​∥ΔM∥2​∥x+Δx∥. Finally ∥M−1∥2​=1/λN​=κ/∥M∥2​. ■

The change ΔM is usually indefinite, so ∥ΔM∥2​ is its largest eigenvalue in absolute value, not its λ1​. The bound is a worst case, nearly reached only when the change pushes the solution along the eigenvector of λN​.

Let C be the correlation matrix of two assets with ρ=0.95, and solve Cx=e1​. Then raise ρ to 0.96, and compare the relative change of the solution with the bound (11.9) for M=C.

Here κ=1.95/0.05=39, and ΔM has 0.01 off the diagonal, with eigenvalues ±0.01, so ∥ΔM∥2​/∥M∥2​=0.01/1.95=0.005128. The bound 39×0.005128=0.2000 is almost reached, because Δx lies along (1,−1)⊤/2​, the eigenvector of the small eigenvalue 1−ρ.

PitfallWhat a condition number does not say

A large κ does not mean that a computed solution is wrong; it says how far the solution could move for a given change in the matrix. Nor does it rank realized changes. S5 has κ(Σ^(1))=10.9 and its solution moved by 0.910, while U28 has κ(Σ^(1))=126 and moved by 0.729 (Exhibit 13).

導讀反矩陣為什麼會放大誤差

由譜分解（spectral decomposition），反矩陣（inverse matrix）Σ^−1 把 b 在每個特徵向量（eigenvector）方向上的分量乘上 1/λk​，特徵值（eigenvalue）越小的方向權重越大。偏偏最小的那些特徵值彼此接近、方向很不穩定，所以解 Σ^x=1 時，矩陣只動了 24.5%，解的相對變動卻是 0.729。條件數（condition number）κ 給的是最壞情況：相對誤差最多放大 κ 倍，28 個產業的上界是 30.93，實際只有 0.729。條件數大不代表算錯，也不能用來排序實際變動的大小。把小特徵值往上拉可以降低條件數，這是 LM30 的主題。

9Eigen-decomposition in Python

calculate the eigenvalues and eigenvectors of a 2×2 symmetric matrix by hand, and of an N×N one with eigh
explain the sign convention for eigenvectors and why near-equal eigenvalues make their eigenvectors unstable

For symmetric matrices, NumPy's eigh returns the eigenvalues in ascending order (NumPy reference), so reverse them, and signs the eigenvectors arbitrarily, so apply the sign rule. It uses an iterative method, not the characteristic polynomial (stated without proof; Golub and Van Loan 2013, ch. 8).

The same components also come from the singular value decomposition of X~/T​ (LM12, (12.5); Strang 2016, §7.3), the route software prefers. The squared singular values are the eigenvalues of Σ^. For correlation PCA, decompose the standardized returns X~diag(s)−1/T​ instead.

np.linalg.cond uses the spectral norm, so it equals λ1​/λN​ only for symmetric positive definite input. Solve systems with solve, never with inv (LM9).

Python
import numpy as np

def eig_desc(M):
    """Eigenpairs of a symmetric M, largest first, signed by the book's rule."""
    lam, Q = np.linalg.eigh(M)              # ascending order
    lam, Q = lam[::-1], Q[:, ::-1].copy()   # largest first
    for k in range(Q.shape[1]):
        a = np.abs(Q[:, k])
        i = np.flatnonzero(a >= a.max() * (1 - 1e-12))[0]   # first of ties
        if Q[i, k] < 0:
            Q[:, k] = -Q[:, k]
    return lam, Q

# X: the T x N array of daily returns (lab, Part 1)
S = np.cov(X, rowvar=False, ddof=0)
lam, Q = eig_desc(np.corrcoef(X, rowvar=False))
print(lam[:2] / lam.sum())                    # explained shares (11.6)
lamS, QS = eig_desc(S)
print(np.linalg.cond(S), lamS[0] / lamS[-1])  # equal: S is SPD
x = np.linalg.solve(S, np.ones(X.shape[1]))   # never inv(S) @ ones

Summary

  • An eigenvector is a direction that a matrix only stretches, Mx=λk​x; the eigenvalues solve det(M−λk​I)=0 (11.1)–(11.2) and add up to the trace.
  • A real symmetric matrix has real eigenvalues and orthonormal eigenvectors (11.3); it is positive semidefinite exactly when every λk​≥0.
  • Every unit direction has a variance between λN​ and λ1​ (11.4). The condition number κ=λ1​/λN​ of the correlation matrix is 70.6 for U28 and 77.0 for 0050 and 00631L.
  • The first principal component maximizes variance over unit vectors, with the eigenvalue as multiplier (11.5). In U28 it carries 52.6% of the standardized variance; 12 components reach 80%.
  • Eigenvectors turn by about the perturbation divided by the gap (11.7). U28's first component is stable (cosine at least 0.98 every year); beyond it, the components of the two halves change places.
  • The inverse weights direction k by 1/λk​ (11.8), so solutions come mostly from small, poorly determined directions. The bound (11.9) is a worst case: 30.93 for U28's halves, against a realized 0.729.
