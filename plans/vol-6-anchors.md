**Common definitions.** E5 (the through case): `vol5_series.frames("E5")`, estimation frame $T=2{,}362$
(2017-01-18 → 2026-10-08), wealth frame 2,368 sessions; the rules' books from `vol5_trades.parquet` (targets and
cost ÷ pre-trade wealth per trade), rebuilt day by day with the through case's recursion and checked against
`vol5_rules.parquet` to 1e-12 (no rule is re-implemented). TR23: `vol3_taiex.parquet` (`tr`, `price`, `R`). TX23:
`vol4_tx23.parquet` (`rF`, `rS`, `F`, `volume`). LM2's sample: `etf_daily_returns.parquet`. T5S: the five named days'
raw files under `data/raw/intraday/taiex5s/` (per-day statistics only). US proxies (pending): `USStockPrice`
`adj_close` of SPY, TLT, GLD, USO and DBO, 2007-07-02 → 2026-10-08, and `TaiwanExchangeRate` USD `spot_buy`,

and `TaiwanStockDividendResult` (label `息`), 2007-07-02 → 2008-12-31, every close checked against the spread in exact
arithmetic (the two ex-dates excepted, their previous close equal to `before_price`) and the dates against TR23's;
total return $(P_t+D_t)/P_{t-1}-1$. **Scenario windows** (TR23): trough = TR23's lowest close of the named year
(2008, 2015, 2020) or of the 20 sessions from the named day (2024-08-05, 2025-04-07); peak = TR23's highest close
after the previous scenario's trough (2008: from TR23's first close) and before the trough; named day = the window's
worst TR23 day; worst $m$ sessions = the worst compound TR23 return over $m$ consecutive sessions ending in
(peak, trough]. **Replays**: frozen = exposures at the window's first close held fixed, loss
$\mathbf f^{\top}\mathbf R^{\mathrm{sc}}$ with compound wealth-frame returns, interest on cash and borrowing left
out; named day = exposures at the previous close times that day's returns; actual = the rule's wealth ratio.
**Proxies**: the TWD value of US session $D$ is its adjusted close times the mid of the first FX row dated after $D$;
a Taiwan date takes the last US session before it (plans/vol-5.md, B5); 0050 is itself in 2008 (its own files; TR23
stands in while they are absent, and the line says so) and in 2015 (LM2's sample). **Printing of proxy numbers**: to
1 dp, flagged "CENT" when a change of one cent in each adjusted close the number uses (first order:
$(1+R)\cdot0.01\,(1/P_a+1/P_b)$ per proxy return, weighted by $\lvert f_i\rvert$ in a book; 0050's and TR23's terms
are exact) could move its first decimal; a CENT value prints to 0 dp, or with "about" when the flag says 0 dp is not
safe either (PITFALLS §3). **Distances**: Mahalanobis $d$ with the full estimation frame's mean $\bar{\mathbf R}$ and
$\hat\Sigma$ (divisor $T$). **Reverse stress**: $\ell=10\%$ in one day; closed form
$\mathbf R^{\mathrm{sc}*}=\bar{\mathbf R}-(\ell+\mathbf f^{\top}\bar{\mathbf R})\hat\Sigma\mathbf f/s_p^2$; bounded
version by cvxpy (tolerances in A35-40), KKT residuals and the closed form with both constraints active checked to
1e-6. Betas: OLS slope with intercept on TR23's same-day $R$. Ruin leverage of a loss $R<0$: $-1/R$. Counts on
two-decimal prices use `Fraction`; counts on computed values use the guard 1e-12. Quantiles `inverted_cdf`.
Rounding: every printed value passes through `f(x, k…)`, which prints $\max k+3$ decimals and flags "NEAR" when $x$
lies within a tenth of a last-digit unit of a rounding boundary at a printed precision $k$; the brief rounds a
flagged value from the unrounded one.
