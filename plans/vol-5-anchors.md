estimation frame. A window is the trailing 504 estimation rows through a signal (`vol5_rules.schedule`, 92 signals,
2019-02-27 → 2026-09-30). Weekly and monthly returns compound the estimation frame's daily returns within ISO weeks
and calendar months; the first and last period, partial, are dropped (500 weeks, 116 months). They annualize with
the periods a year as LM19 counts them (lm19.md:39): $A_{\text{week}}=500/9.6975=51.56$, the weeks with a session
over the years spanned by the 506 calendar weeks from 2017-01-23 to 2026-10-04 (six Lunar New Year weeks have no
session), and 12 months. Simple returns throughout; every moment divides by $T$ (`cov0`, `ddof=0`). A
decomposition of weights $\mathbf w$ under a covariance matrix $S$ uses $\operatorname{Cov}(R_i,R_p)=(S\mathbf w)_i$,
$\beta_{i,p}=(S\mathbf w)_i/\mathbf w^{\top}S\mathbf w$, $\mathrm{MRC}_i=(S\mathbf w)_i/\sqrt{\mathbf w^{\top}S\mathbf w}$,
$\mathrm{RC}_i=w_i\mathrm{MRC}_i$ and share $=\mathrm{RC}_i/s_p=w_i\beta_{i,p}$; printed in annual units with $A\hat\Sigma$.
