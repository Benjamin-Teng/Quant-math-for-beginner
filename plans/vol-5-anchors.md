# Volume V — anchors

Every number the Volume V briefs (`plans/vol-5.md`) quote, with the definition that reproduces it. Each module's
`data/vol5_anchors_lmNN.py` prints its rows (`PYTHONUTF8=1 uv run python data/vol5_anchors_lm27.py`), after
`data/vol3_series.py` (TR23), `data/vol2_series.py` (U28), `data/vol5_series.py` (E5 and the checkpoint funds) and
`data/vol5_rules.py` (the through case) have run. IDs are global (A27-01 … A31-nn) and are cited the same
way in the briefs, the reviews and the numbers scripts; each module's `data/lmNN_numbers.py` compares its values with
these rows and reports a difference rather than forcing it. A withdrawn anchor keeps its row. From Stage 3 the module
sources are authoritative (preface of `plans/vol-5.md`).

Status: revised to the editor's decisions of 2026-10-24 (first revision); the anchors a revision added or changed await their independent recomputation.

**Common definitions.** Sample E5 (`data/vol5_series.py`'s `frames("E5")` and `ohlc()`, reading
`data/processed/vol5_frames.parquet` and `vol5_ohlc.parquet`; FinMind `TaiwanStockPrice`, `TaiwanStockDividendResult`
and `TaiwanStockSplitPrice`, retrieved 2026-10-24 as `data/README.md` records): 0050, 00646, 00679B, 00635U and
00642U, daily total returns $(P_t+D_t)/(P_{t-1}/s_t)-1$, $s_t$ the split ratio (4 for 0050 on 2025-06-18, else 1),
from the base close 2017-01-17 to 2026-10-08; years = calendar days ÷ 365.25 (3,551 ÷ 365.25). The estimation frame
keeps the 2,362 dates on which every fund has a one-session return and annualizes with
$A=T\times365.25/\text{days}=2{,}362\times365.25/3{,}551=242.951$; the wealth frame keeps all 2,368 TR23 sessions
after the base, a halted fund earning 0 and its spanning return booked when it trades again ($A_{\mathrm w}=243.569$).
"Full sample" means the estimation frame. A window is the trailing 504 estimation rows through a signal
(`vol5_rules.schedule`, 92 signals, 2019-02-27 → 2026-09-30), its covariance annualized with the full sample's $A$.
Weekly and monthly returns compound the estimation frame's daily returns within ISO weeks (keys ISO year, ISO week)
and calendar months; the first and last period, in which the sample starts and ends, are dropped (500 weeks, 116
calendar months). They annualize with the periods a year as LM19 counts them (lm19.md:39):
$A_{\text{week}}=500\times365.25/3{,}542=51.56$, the weeks with a session over the 3,542 days of the 506 calendar
weeks from 2017-01-23 to 2026-10-04 (six Lunar New Year weeks have no session), and 12 months. Simple returns
throughout; every moment divides by $T$ (`cov0`, `ddof=0`). A decomposition of weights $\mathbf w$ under a covariance
matrix $S$ uses $\operatorname{Cov}(R_i,R_p)=(S\mathbf w)_i$, $\beta_{i,p}=(S\mathbf w)_i/\mathbf w^{\top}S\mathbf w$,
$\mathrm{MRC}_i=(S\mathbf w)_i/\sqrt{\mathbf w^{\top}S\mathbf w}$, $\mathrm{RC}_i=w_i\mathrm{MRC}_i$ and share
$=\mathrm{RC}_i/s_p=w_i\beta_{i,p}$; printed in annual units with $A\hat\Sigma$. R1 and R2 come from
`vol5_rules.signals` and `wealth_path` (costs as plans/vol-5.md, "Cost model": commission 0.1425% each side, tax 0.1%
on sales of every fund but 00679B); a "pre-trade weight" is a fund's value over pre-trade wealth. A correlation's
normal-theory standard error is $(1-\hat\rho^2)/\sqrt T$ and its plug-in one $\operatorname{sd}(L_t)/\sqrt T$ (LM20,
Practice Problem 6, lm20.md:558). U28 (`data/lm10_series.py`'s `u28()`; FinMind `TaiwanStockPrice`, retrieved
2026-10-11, copied closes repaired): 28 sector price indices and the TAIEX price index, $T=4{,}382$, $A=245.4794$;
the market model is LM23's (23.1) with an intercept, residual covariances divide by $T$. Problems 1–4, 6 and 10 use
the hypothetical inputs their rows state. Numbers printed by published modules are echoed with their file:line and
